## Core distinction

An **unmodified opinion** means financial statements were fairly presented in all material respects under the audit's scope; it does not guarantee every procurement decision complied with law, that internal controls were flawless, or that every service had its promised outcome. A GFOA award for reporting does not serve as an independent all-purpose certification of fiscal practices. Examine separate Single Audit, Government Auditing Standards reporting, schedules of findings, management responses, prior-year findings, corrective-action plans, Council packets, and actual contract/payment records.

## Questions requiring primary records

1. **Financial statement opinion vs compliance:** identify audit period, auditor firm, full opinion, every GAGAS and federal award finding, reference number, questioned costs if any, management response, prior-year recurrence, and completion evidence. Never import another city's findings.
2. **Budget transfers:** obtain authorizing ordinances, fund restriction instruments, entry-date general ledger and actual expenditure records; distinguish authorized reclassification, restricted fund substitution, advance, temporary loan, interfund transfer and permanent diversion.
3. **Purchase orders:** compare requisition → encumbrance → contract → invoice → approval → payment; calculate elapsed dates without treating delays alone as illicit.
4. **Council oversight:** request presentation slides, meeting recording/minutes, management letter availability, auditor engagement scope and which specific questions were answered. Do not infer deliberate withholding without documented disclosure duty and evidence.
5. **Grant transparency:** resolve award ID and Assistance Listing, pass-through chain, FSRS/subaward filing duties by relevant period, financial corrections and grantor acceptance. An audit finding is not necessarily a quantified loss.
6. **Lease and enforcement claims:** maintain separate project-specific packets for airport leases, museum/historical lease, code enforcement, sales-tax assessments, and economic incentives; obtain comparator decisions and lawful criteria before any inference of selective treatment.
7. **Independent responses:** record City and County interpretations, auditor explanation, agency responses, and corrections alongside critical testimony.

## Accounting lineages

For every investigated amount use: **estimated → proposed → adopted/appropriated → transferred/amended → legally obligated → invoiced → paid → reconciled/audited → independently measured outcome**. Record fund, fiscal year, source restriction, agreement, decision maker, recipient, date, document URL, and evidentiary confidence. No double counting across City, County, district or pass-through.

## Publication discipline

Separate VERIFIED RECORD, REPORTED CLAIM, Q ANALYSIS, UNRESOLVED, DISPUTED, and CORRECTED states; a missing record is not proof of wrongdoing. Explicitly display audit vintage (FY2024 ≠ FY2025 ≠ FY2026), independent auditor's scope, and current correction status. Public updates require accountable human approval.
