## Lake Pueblo model (illustrative, not a forecast)

Formula: **incremental local gross sales = 3,009,507 × converted visits share × additional qualifying local spend per converted visit**.

| Scenario | Converted visits | Extra spending per converted visit | Hypothetical gross local sales |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Conservative | 5% | $25 | $3,761,884 |
| Working | 15% | $40 | $18,057,042 |
| Stretch | 30% | $60 | $54,171,126 |

This is **not public revenue**, jobs, profit, a return-on-investment estimate, or an estimate of *new* tourism spending without a verified baseline. The model must exclude displacement and double-counting.

## Candidate pilots and requirements

1. **Workforce access:** compare actual district offerings, waiting lists, transport access, qualified instructors, credentials, apprenticeships and post-school outcomes; pilot paid/credit-bearing local placements under school and family safeguards.
2. **Lake-to-city commerce:** partner with Colorado Parks and Wildlife, Visit Pueblo, merchants and food producers for voluntary referrals into Pueblo; measure incremental local sales, environmental costs and accessibility.
3. **Eastside property rehabilitation:** distinguish grants sought / awarded / paid / spent / inspected; track ownership, environmental hazards, community consent, procurement, condition after remediation, affordability and displacement.
4. **Civic feedback:** survey specific projects with representative sampling; publish corrections and nonresponse limitations. Do not infer general preferences from a social thread.

## Digital twin / Q10 integration contract

**Past–present–future:** Baseline historical measures, current verified measurements, scenarios and opinions must use distinct data types and view labels.  
**Child impact (mandatory):** coursework access, youth transportation and paid-work safety, accessible recreation, housing hazards and anti-displacement. No student-level data in this packet.  
**Follow the money:** distinguish obligation → appropriation → award → disbursement → expenditure → delivered asset → independently measured benefit.  
**Signals, currently NOT activated:** CPW annual visitation, D60/D70 offering changes, destination-plan milestones, public grants and inspection completions, evidence corrections.  
**Risk controls:** avoid false allegations, demographic or political profiling, political targeting, private-source disclosure, unapproved model operation and automated governance. Human override, privacy review, source corrections and shutdown must remain available.  
**TTL guidance:** annually for park visitation (or upon revision); term-by-term for course availability; monthly/award-time for grants; immediate on significant source correction. TTL is a refresh target, not evidence of active monitoring.  
**Audit rule:** change requests and disagreements append dated amendments, not silent mutation. Versioned immutable evidence manifest and document identifiers connect the controlled archive to a public redacted synopsis.
