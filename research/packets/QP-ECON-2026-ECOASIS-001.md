## 4. Due-diligence questions by accountable system
**Developer, PEDCO and capital:** Who is the actual project sponsor and its full legal entity chain? Are land options held? What total capital is committed versus being raised? Who are the lenders, equity investors, greenhouse EPC/O&M contractors, and anchor buyers? What are the conditions precedent, bond/security, step-in rights and abandonment obligations?

**Land and land-use authority:** Identify parcel numbers and owner(s); City vs County zoning; annexation history; agricultural/industrial classifications; protected habitats; nearby residences; floodplain, soils and drainage; road access; sewer/wastewater and emergency-response capacity; development hearings and permit IDs. No GIS polygon should be published until coordinates are documentary.

**Water and food systems:** Identify supplying utility or decreed water right; expected annual acre-feet; peak and seasonal demand; consumptive use vs withdrawals; irrigation recirculation, storage and discharge; drought contingency; water quality; product destinations and realistic local-food share.

**Power and climate:** Annual and peak MWh/MW, heating and cooling fuels, utility capacity study, line/substation upgrades, backup power, emissions, snow/wind loads, light/glare and waste handling. Any proposed solar generation is not an installed asset.

**Jobs and residents:** For each phase separate construction jobs, permanent direct FTE, part-time/seasonal, indirect and induced jobs; verify annual wages and benefits, local-hire measures, training costs, accessibility, job retention and automation exposure. A projected 1,000+ is neither a minimum guarantee nor an observed payroll count.

**Public-money ledger:** Disaggregate land discounts; direct grants; tax credits; property tax abatements; bonds; state/federal aid; half-cent funds; utility or road/site improvements; foregone revenue; performance security and clawbacks. Track **proposed → authorized → contracted → disbursed → spent → independently verified outcomes**. Record issuer, fund, fiscal year, award ID and source.

**Child/household impacts:** Track household income, work schedules, transportation, housing, public school/CTE opportunities, agricultural-worker protections and long-run resource resilience; do not collect individual child records.
