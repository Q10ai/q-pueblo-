# Q-Pueblo story-first answer standard v0.1
Purpose: answer the actual question in understandable language; evidence before presentation. Narrative may help comprehension and engagement but can persuade beyond the evidence. Never fabricate a character, scene, quote, motive, cause, trend, or result. Use documented experience or explicitly hypothetical examples.

Research basis: Dahlstrom (2014), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25225368/; Digital.gov plain language https://digital-gov-static-prod.app.cloud.gov/guides/plain-language/principles/organize; GOV.UK clear structure https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/writing-guidelines/clear-structure/; NIH https://www.nih.gov/institutes-nih/nih-office-director/office-communications-public-liaison/clear-communication/plain-language-nih/plain-language-getting-started-or-brushing-up/presenting-your-information.

## Contract
1. Scope lock: exact question, canonical identity, geography, time, requested outcome. Related topic is not a substitute answer.
2. LL recover original work first; retain earlier versions and story rights; GLEAN only missing primary evidence.
3. Answer lead: what is known, the date and the central significance. No procedural filler.
4. Context: define terms and explain in concrete language why this matters.
5. Chronology: dated decisions and actions, not generic history.
6. Who did what: source-supported role and action; distinguish responsibilities from associations, no motive inference.
7. Numbers: units, time, financial stage and denominator. Keep separate projects and budgets separate.
8. Human and system effects: observed vs intended vs conditional; consent-cleared experience, never manufactured anecdotes.
9. Other viewpoints and alternatives: actual documented perspectives or clearly labeled analytical options, no false balance.
10. Q assessment: explain implications and options, EE/RX/Child Core/BACKUP/LEAP where relevant; no fabricated evaluations.
11. Unknowns and what next: say what would establish missing facts; date-sensitive actionable sources.
12. Sources and expandable technical layer: 40 perspectives are supporting depth, not mandatory front-facing boilerplate. For simple questions use direct answer first; for urgent or procedural questions put safe action first.
## Templates
Living issue/project: answer, origins, timeline, roles, money, effects, alternatives, next milestones, sources.
Historical: context, chronology, primary artifacts, documented voices, disputed interpretations, legacy.
Procedure: direct action, eligibility, responsible office, documents, cost/deadline, exceptions, alternatives, source date.
Entity: identity, duties, documented actions, connections, contacts, role history and sources.
Rumor: exact claim, evidence outcome, explanation, uncertainty, other perspectives and why it matters; do not repeat harmful allegations unnecessarily.
Scenario: assumptions, calculation, ranges, limits, alternatives and fallback; no prediction presented as observation.
## Audit requirements
Exact-identity relevance, claim/source coverage, dated freshness, no unsupported causal bridge, financial stage integrity, no invented human story, plain language, actionable answer, accessible display, contradiction/uncertainty, privacy, reproducible citation. Test task comprehension, source finding, calibrated confidence, and response time with real audiences; not engagement alone. No user testing or causal performance claim in this candidate.
