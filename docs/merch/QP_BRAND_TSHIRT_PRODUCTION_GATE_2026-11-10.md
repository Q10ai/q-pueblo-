## Context
A proposed black shirt uses the existing Q-Pueblo image system: circular turquoise-and-gold Q, Pueblo civic/industrial/river illustration, chile accents, gold typography and mountain sunset. The current concept places the art on the mid/lower chest rather than crowding the collar. The candidate slogan is **“Get to Know Pueblo. Help Shape Its Future.”**

## Identity consistency
- **Approved website logo:** `public/assets/q-pueblo-approved-logo.jpg`; **approved banner:** `public/assets/q-pueblo-header-wide.png`. Do not silently replace them.

- Preserve Q proportions, readable spelling, one coherent brand lockup and explicit non-government affiliation.
