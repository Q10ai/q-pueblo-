# Multiple POV + History of Histories + scenario evolution

Status: DESIGN CANDIDATE. Applies to Q10 and Q-Pueblo projections; no new canon authority.

## Claims and voices are separate objects
A perspective is a source-attributed view, experience or purpose-built synthetic analytic lens, not an alternative factual record. One person's experience can be accurately recorded without establishing that a general explanation or accusation is true. Preserve consent, redaction and the opportunity to challenge a characterization. Do not infer moral or criminal status from a graph, opinion or third-party repost.

For each perspective store source lineage, attribution/permission, known date, world-effective period, evidence status, counterevidence, limitations, security classification, child impact (past/present/future), TTL review trigger, review receipt, and revision/supersession links. If a perspective is fictional, label it at each display layer, not just the JSON metadata.

## Historical replay contract
`as_of` means which conditions or version is applicable to the real-world date. `known_at` means what information the research system had received by that date. Queries that use both dates may reveal what was reasonably believed at the time without rewriting later knowledge into the earlier period. Do not silently delete mistakes; append corrections with reason, source, authorized reviewer and current state. Retain audit hashes unless a valid privacy deletion/retention law requires a separately governed treatment.

## Five stakeholder lenses
Family/child, workforce, business/service, government/budget and environment/future are useful recurring *questions* to ask, not fixed interest groups or actual spokespersons. More lenses can be added for impacted communities, disability/accessibility, public safety, labor, neighborhoods and regional dependencies where evidence supports the need. No one lens is assumed to represent a demographic group.

## Probability and future truth
A hypothetical branch is never treated as measured outcomes. If probabilities are not calibrated to an appropriate baseline and validated, store `NOT_ESTIMATED`, not fabricated numeric rankings. Q10's quantum-related work is a separate governed engineering subject; the current branches here do not execute quantum circuits or imply quantum advantage. Futures change when source versions, policy decisions, constraints or observed outcomes change, subject to human review.

## Change-control example (synthetic)
- `2026-01-01` fictional hypothesis: a street needs urgent work; status HYPOTHESIS.
- `2026-02-01` fictional preliminary inspection is entered; claim remains unverified pending original record.
- `2026-03-01` fictional maintenance log supports a narrower issue; earlier broad claim is linked as SUPERSEDED, not silently erased.
- Future alternatives are recalculated as *new speculative versions*; existing scenario receipts remain available.

## Blocking rules
No child/vulnerable exposure evaluation; no legal reviewer where necessary; unlicensed source; identity ambiguity; missing original timestamp; private household location; unverifiable accusation; or unresolved high-severity security defect => HOLD. The fact that a narrative is instructive, popular or technically attractive is not a waiver.
