# Q Pueblo — User-First Site Architecture and Context-First Tile Standard
**Version:** QP-UX-MASTER.v1.0
**Prepared:** 2026-11-21
**Status:** editorial and engineering blueprint; does not authorize deployment
**Purpose:** reconcile every requested subject, KOS, LL/Quilt/Twin, finance, learning, tools and public presentation without empty sections or duplicated evidence

## Governing decision

**Context is the product.** Enter through a familiar subject or an idea; receive the most complete defensible, understandable KOS account available at that moment. After the readable story, present only actions genuinely relevant to that subject. Do not send residents first to a tools workbench, a research prompt, 40 template fields, unfilled content slots, or a collection of questions they must research themselves.

Core engine: Q10/Q Core → sourced crosswalk → Qification → natural, tenth-grade-level contextual story → optional relevant actions and learning. In the current public beta Qify is a local matcher and the on-demand research gateway is OFF. Navigation changes must never imply that live production is already available.

## Navigation and homepage information order

Top navigation (small): Home | What's Happening | Explore & Ask Q | Learn Pueblo | Find Help | About. Main subject tiles carry other pathways; no primary Tools nav or separate "Q Tools" promotional section.

1. **What's Happening Now:** latest editorially verified developments first, dates and deadlines, high human relevance, brief contextual summaries, selected substantive tiles. Rotating the cards must not imply real-time verification. Time-sensitive emergencies link to real authorities rather than self-generated warnings.
2. **Major Stories & Investigations:** one lead full KOS paper plus a few substantial verified stories. Headlines describe what matters, not internal report counts. Old sources are not secretly promoted to current findings.
3. **Explore Pueblo + Ask Q:** one clear field with subject/idea input, immediately followed by eight original illustrated gateway tiles, ordered for everyday residents; category → subcategory → Enter KOS. The same KOS search must accept any named subject or idea.
4. **Help, Services & Opportunities:** practical local support, eligibility, location/hours/contact, last-confirmed date, no inferred openings or eligibility, and verified provider links. Direct routes for basic needs, grandparents as parents, children/youth, seniors, disability and veteran services.
5. **Learn Pueblo — History of Histories, Culture & Play:** LL connected to every subject and all generations. Research, original documents, oral histories, museums, churches, field learning, lesson guides, and games with five levels × at least three worthwhile challenges each when completed. Learning is not only quizzes.
6. **Who, What & Where:** people, offices, roles, organizations, agencies, funders, institutions, neighborhoods, projects and financial relationships. Filterable source-verified three-tier entity hierarchy and linked issue/study inventory, not a loose name dump.
7. **Pueblo's Future & Solutions:** already effective, upcoming confirmed and proposed state/federal changes; financial and societal impact, genuine options, responsible authorities, timelines, LEAP, Twin scenarios and SHOCK/BACKUP testing. Consequence estimates are labeled scenarios.
8. **About Q Pueblo:** how KOS works, current vs designed capabilities, privacy/corrections, public provenance, glossary, professional/user handbooks, appendices, methods, release and accountability. Do not foreground Q10 internals to nontechnical readers.

### Persistent architecture

- One single canonical evidence/claim identity, one canonical subject record, one canonical entity/role ID (effective dated), one issue ID and one study ID; many navigational views and related stories.
- KOS is always available at family and subtopic entry; a link to the KOS entry does NOT establish live generation or completed paper status.
- The 25 family names are editorial navigation, not a demand for 25 empty reports. Exhaust a subject when verified evidence supports it and reuse connections to avoid repeating source collection.
- Priority is determined by user impact, urgency, recent decisions, public money, safety and service needs, not internally mandated report lengths or number of evidence slots.
- TTL latest verified facts first, with older versions preserved for LL. Date of event, document issuance, retrieval/verification, effective date and review due are distinct fields.

## Detailed subcategory coverage requirements

1. **Housing & Community:**
   - Housing: affordability, rental and ownership, eviction, homelessness and transitional housing, property quality, planning/zoning, construction, neighborhoods and displacement.
   - Social: grandparents raising grandchildren, kinship caregiving, custody/legal care, financial and school support, child welfare, drug and alcohol use/overdose/recovery, mental health, poverty, food insecurity, youth activities, seniors, veterans, disability, victim support and intergenerational issues.
   - Culture: Indigenous and living cultural communities, Hispanic/Chicano/African American/immigrant histories; Steelworks, El Pueblo, Sangre de Cristo/Buell, da Vinci, Pueblo Heritage, Rosemount, aviation and other museums; churches, faith communities, buildings, cemeteries, oral history, arts, festivals, music, archives, nonprofits and funding. Religion covered neutrally, no private member profiles.
   - Recreation: sports and youth leagues, nature, parks, trails, tourism, events and access.
   - Help: verified assistance, local providers, operating hours, contact/location and accessibility, claims and obligations documented, not invented.

2. **Money, Business & Work:**
   - Finance: household through regional/state/federal funds; approved vs contracted vs paid vs delivered, debt, grants, tax, liabilities, bonds, providers, nonprofits, private development, infrastructure lifecycle. LL to LEAP.
   - Economy: economic development and incentives, steel/manufacturing, business attraction vs actual projects, wages/benefits, labor, small businesses, commerce, workers, factories, investment and workforce education.
   - Agriculture: Pueblo chile, farms/ranches, irrigation, water/land/soil, food systems, workers, food processing, trade, drought and agricultural heritage.

3. **Education, Health & Opportunity:**
   - Healthcare: hospitals, clinics, public health, Medicaid/Medicare, insurance, mental health, addiction medicine, children/maternal care, seniors/veterans, workforce, providers and funding, availability/quality only where current records exist.
   - Education: early learning, K-12/D60/D70, disability/special education, CSU Pueblo, PCC, vocational education, lifelong learning, finances and outcomes.
   - History & learning: comprehensive regional chronology and historiography, whose evidence survived, source conflicts, LL and history of every subject, educational projects, links and 5 levels × 3 minimum for complete games.

4. **Government, Law & Accountability:**
   - Local governance: neighborhood to municipality/county/district/state/federal roles, elected/appointed officials, transition dates, boards, departments, public meetings, budgets, grants and cross-entity roles.
   - State/federal impacts: changes in law, funding, benefits, education, taxes, environment, labor, housing, AI and insurance; classify enacted/effective/upcoming/proposed/challenged and explicitly calculate Pueblo effects only with evidence.
   - Legal: rights, courts, procedural justice, civil and criminal law, public records, administrative remedies, contracts, governance, legal aid and historical changes.

5. **Water, Environment & Energy:**
   - Water, air, soil, habitats, ecosystems, parks, conservation, remediation, climate and drought/wildfire.
   - Utilities: water delivery, wastewater, treatment, electric and gas generation/distribution, rate proposals vs approvals, reliability, money and public health.
   - Legacy environment and industry connections, water rights, agriculture and energy transition; local infrastructure dependencies.

6. **Safety & Resilience:**
   - Law enforcement: agencies and jurisdictions, public safety, police/sheriff, jails and corrections, detention staff, overtime and compensation, crime and reporting definitions, victims, rights/oversight, emergency response.
   - SHOCK: hazard/stress register, financial/legal/technology/natural cascades; real independent backup chains and recovery, not untested infinite failovers.
   - Conflict and solutions: family, youth, workplace, neighborhood, resource, legal and civic disputes; evidence, parties, authority, safe remedies, mediation when voluntary and appropriate, costed options and actual outcomes.

7. **Infrastructure & Transportation:**
   - Roads, rail, airport, public transit, bridges, sidewalks, water and sewer assets, electric grid and broadband, buildings, public facilities, factories, waste, stormwater, housing-serving infrastructure.
   - Regional geography: Pueblo city and neighborhoods, Pueblo West, rural communities, small towns, regional basins and institutional service boundaries, demographic change.
   - Each asset: owner, operator, condition, age, capacity, maintenance, contracts, costs, inspections, historical hazards, interconnected failure and planned future.

8. **AI, Information & Future Systems:**
   - Information/media: journalism, community media, public notices, archives, source checking, misinformation, privacy and communication infrastructure.
   - AI era: jobs, education, government, industry, health, utilities, resources, data centers, environmental costs, workforce and future planning.
   - AI governance: constitutional/human rights, data privacy, child safety, regulation, human oversight, public procurement, emergency safeguards, audits and rollback.
   - Q/KOS: core truth/provenance → crosswalk → Qification → complete paper; actual versus designed, Child Zero, EE, T2Y, Twin, Quilt, LL, TTL, LEAP, copies/versions, glossaries and reader/professional handbooks.

## Embedded action placements (replaces the separate tools front door)

Never show a resident EE/T2Y formulas, route cost weights, a developer prompt generator, a bare empty crosswalk or an initial 40-perspective form. Present natural reader labels in context:
- **Read the full KOS story** (default primary action in every subject)
- **Find a service** (Housing, Social, Health, Education, Safety)
- **Follow the money** (Finance, Utilities, Infrastructure, Government, Health, Education, Culture)
- **Check a claim** (Now, Stories, Government, Media, Safety, all consequential subjects)
- **Compare the options** (Conflicts, Housing, Energy, Infrastructure, Health, Development, AI)
- **See who is involved** (all, backed by proven dated entity relations)
- **See the historical timeline** (all subjects through LL)
- **Explore what might happen** (Twin/LEAP; only bounded scenario calculations)
- **Prepare for disruptions** (SHOCK, Safety, Energy, Healthcare, Infrastructure, Social services)
- **Learn or try an activity** (every subject where actual complete educational content exists)
- **Check the sources / suggest a correction** (every substantive paper)

Existing `q-tools.html`, `decision-tools.html`, `twin.html`, `qify.html` can survive as secondary deep links for experts and backward compatibility. Remove the main navigation Tools label and homepage generic Tools strip **only after** every supported useful action is placed inside the relevant categories and tested. Internal controls are not deleted.

## What a KOS subject view looks like

**Subject page:**
1. A useful, nontechnical headline answering the topic.
2. A few-sentence direct summary and current verified date.
3. **One continuous, fully developed narrative**: origin/history, chronology, people, offices, decisions, economics and figures, concrete human and regional effects, disputed evidence, current status, actual choices and accountable next steps. No prescribed number of filler headings.
4. Source-linked facts, current-to-historic timelines and actual contextual crosswalk connections; dated links beneath/alongside—not replacing the story.
5. Only relevant actions and learning products (above).
6. Technical source ledger, finance tracing, maps, graph relationships, assumptions, versions and corrections accessible on demand to those who want them.

For user-entered requests, KOS resolves subject → connected existing sources → original verification → Q Core decision/rights gate → crosswalk → tenth-grade-level Qified narrative. Publish no invented numbers, private personal profiles, unsupported associations or fabricated quotes. Unknown facts remain unknown without injecting long gap lists into the public reading flow.

**User-visible modes:** If human-reviewed story exists, show it. If verified KOS generation is enabled, synthesize an on-demand provisional paper with sentence/paragraph-level source mapping and a final human publication gate. If live research is unavailable, clearly show only the already verified narrower material; never advertise a directory lookup as a newly generated paper.

## Acceptance conditions

P0 — truth, non-confusion and navigation:
- Every original tile has a clear pathway to all assigned families and their subtopics; 25 primary families / 77 initially registered entry topics remain findable, not necessarily exhaustive.
- All routes, old and new, have stable working anchors; old tool links remain compatible until embedded replacement is accepted.
- No live AI claims without a real configured backend; all dated facts and source links checked as appropriate.
- No duplicate major articles or links claiming full evidence solely because a report is in the catalogue.
- An independent reader can locate a major story or family through one or two intuitive choices.

P1 — KOS narrative completeness:
- Source-checked story has source IDs, factual chronology, context, entity roles, financial stages where material, rights and consequences, feasible choices and verification/correction pathways.
- The story reads as coherent prose at about 10th grade; no forced 40-part filler, no sections consisting mainly of research requests, no internal meta language.
- User-selected question scopes the paper; do not deliver a nearby topic or generic family boilerplate as a complete answer.
- Sitewide TTL, LL, crosswalking, T2Y/EE/Child Zero, SHOCK and backups applied internally and disclosed briefly only when essential to understanding.

P1 — embedded features:
- Each tool action appears in its actual relevant subject, works, has safe defaults, distinguishes real data from examples, returns useful explanation (not technical scores alone), and has a usable exit.
- The same canonical evidence can yield story, source view, financial view, LL lesson, Quilt connections, Twin scenario and user-specific learning, with no drift.
- For all ages, education combines reading, activity, research, history, resources and complete 5-level games where available.

P2 — release:
- Unit/static/route/ARIA/mobile/keyboard audits and privacy/security scanning pass.
- Editor reviews high-stakes claims and current local resources (contacts, eligibility, hours, effective dates).
- Site owner approves controlled merge and deployment; exact deployed commit verified and rollback remains available.

## Build sequence and human release gate

1. Approve the consolidated information hierarchy and preserve art and design assets. This file is a blueprint; review first.
2. Refactor homepage: eliminate duplicate entrances, old Tools nav and generic Tools promo **after** migrated tools work.
3. Make every gateway category page display its assigned families and subcategories with Enter KOS.
4. Embed natural actions in each family and relevant KOS story, starting with help, money, claims, relationships and history.
5. Improve KOS paper consistency across source-backed content and build genuine on-demand source validation in a bounded shadow environment before any live release.
6. Complete deep dives and learning products by demonstrated public need, not forced quotas.
7. Human editorial/safety, browser/visual and production readback.

**No user can be required to know Q terminology to understand Pueblo. Q performs the complex work; users receive the clear result.**
