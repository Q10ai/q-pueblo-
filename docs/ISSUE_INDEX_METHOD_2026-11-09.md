## Material limitation
Does not reconcile post-October 16 developments, all private Q10 packets, original source bytes, GIS, people/entities, live meetings, election certifications, contradictions or outcomes. Candidate topic keyword matches are not proof of identity. Human review before public release of new facts and figures.

## Update process
A genuine authoritative issue count must be generated from the reconciled approved canonical registry with version, aliases, dedup decisions, provenance and stage, not inferred from number of PDFs, chats, source publishers, agency names or news stories. The site count remains explicitly a dated public snapshot until then.
