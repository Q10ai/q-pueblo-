Proposed future editorial lifecycle
How an observation becomes reliable shared knowledge
01
Receive & protect
Check scope, provenance, legal rights, privacy and manipulation risks. Sensitive material stays out of public datasets.
02
Challenge & corroborate
Compare origin records, dates, government authority, opposing evidence and the consequences of error.
03
Amend & preserve
A human editor accepts, rejects or qualifies the claim, appends a dated explanation and leaves a reversible audit trail.
