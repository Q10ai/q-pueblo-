What is Q?

People need information to govern themselves and make better decisions. Q serves residents, families, elected officials, public employees, businesses and community leaders. The same evidence supports everyone; people retain authority.

Q-Pueblo applies a truth-first, human-governed approach to civic knowledge: evidence, alternatives and practical consequences are made visible, while people make decisions. Learn about Q10 and responsible AI · Method and privacy.

About this project

Q-Pueblo is an independent civic governance intelligence project. Its purpose is to help people understand and evaluate how institutions, decisions, resources and outcomes connect. Live AI research and a live city model are not connected to this website. About and corrections.
