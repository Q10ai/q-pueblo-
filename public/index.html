Important participation boundaries

  • No personal allegations, private medical details, children's identifying information or unpublished accusations.
  • GitHub issues are public and require a GitHub account. Do not share secrets or confidential files there.
  • No live private submission database or automatic publication pipeline is enabled.
  • Organized or repeated comments are one or more viewpoints, not proof of majority opinion.
  • Human reviewers must approve consequential changes before they become published findings.

How corrections should proceed

  1. Receive a specific, attributable claim
  2. Quarantine unsafe content
  3. Locate source versions and opposing evidence
  4. Propose an amendment with rationale
  5. Human review, dated publication, and rollback
Q-PUEBLO • MISSION & LIMITS

Public transparency, not public authority

Q-Pueblo organizes research for people who want to understand governance. It is independent of government and does not speak for elected officials or agencies.

What Q-Pueblo does

  • Explain laws, budgets, public decisions and agencies in accessible language
  • Link original records, identify uncertainty and document corrections
  • Trace documented financial and institutional relationships
  • Compare evidence, perspectives, options and conditional impacts
  • Maintain human consent, review, override and audit controls

What it does not do

  • Act as a government agency, legal advisor, auditor or court
  • Endorse or oppose candidates, campaigns or policy choices
  • Make autonomous government, spending or enforcement decisions
  • Publish an allegation as fact because it is repeated or popular
  • Build political persuasion profiles or identify private citizens for targeting

Verification, TTL, EE and T2Y

TTL: Each record has its own review cadence and expiry policy. Outdated source snapshots remain dated and are not silently repackaged as current. RX: Competing explanations receive fair, evidence-based scrutiny. EE: Use the least costly intelligence adequate to the task, falling back to source links when AI capacity is unavailable. T2Y: Prefer research that improves verified public understanding and usable outcomes without sacrificing truth, safety, reversibility or human agency.

Publication gate: Human review is required before consequential findings, sensitive corrections or new evidence claims are promoted. The present website is a public beta and does not imply completion of Q10's full internal evidence reconciliation.
