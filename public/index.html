What does Pueblo County's 2027 budget proposal mean for services?

The posted County presentation compares a $255.83 million 2026 budget with a $217.28 million 2027 proposal before recommended General Fund reductions: a $38.55 million difference, about 15.1%. This is a budget comparison, not $38.55 million of achieved savings or a final adopted plan.

Read the findings

Where can Pueblo households get help with food?

Pueblo County lists emergency food assistance at no cost for qualifying households. Text FOOD to 1-877-644-3663 to receive an intake survey link. Seniors age 60 or older may also qualify for Everyday Eats; SNAP is a separate application and EBT benefit.

Read the findings

How can families apply for District 60 preschool in 2026–27?

District 60's 2026–27 guide directs four-year-old families to complete Colorado Universal Preschool and District 60 enrollment, then wait for placement. Since April 1, enrollment is direct through the district when seats exist. Call Early Childhood at 719-253-6016. Three-year-old programs have separate qualifying criteria.

Read the findings

What has Pueblo County reported about Aspen Acres recovery?

Pueblo County's recovery dashboard reports 453 households in temporary lodging, 36 dumpsters and 81 state resource requests. Its September 8 financial detail identifies $321,833.63 in direct County response and recovery costs. These are published snapshots, not a November 14 cumulative total, a current containment update or final cost audit.
