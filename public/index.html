What does the County's 2027 proposed budget mean?

The posted presentation compares $255.83 million for 2026 with $217.28 million proposed for 2027: a $38.55 million difference, not achieved savings or a final adopted budget. Proposed Road & Bridge funding falls from $22.34M to $19.34M; Human Services from $55.71M to $54.79M.

Source checked November 14, 2026 · Recheck due November 15.

Where can households get food assistance?

For a free prepared meal, Pueblo Community Soup Kitchen lists 422 W. 7th St., Monday–Saturday, 8 a.m.–noon. For groceries, Cooperative Care lists 326 W. 8th St., Monday–Thursday, 9 a.m.–1 p.m. at 719-543-7484. Confirm hours before travel; call 211 for alternatives.

Source checked November 15, 2026 · Recheck due November 22.

How does preschool enrollment work?

District 60 directs four-year-old families to complete Colorado Universal Preschool and district enrollment. Seats are subject to availability; call Early Childhood at 719-253-6016. Three-year-old programs have separate eligibility.

Source checked November 14, 2026 · Recheck due November 21.

Read the enrollment guide

Aspen Acres recovery

The County's September 8 financial detail identifies $321,833.63 in direct County response and recovery costs. The dashboard also reports 453 households in temporary lodging and 81 state resource requests. These are historical snapshots, not current occupancy, confirmed approvals or a final audit.

Source checked November 14, 2026 · Recheck due November 21.
