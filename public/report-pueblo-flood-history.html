The 1921 Pueblo flood: connecting historical evidence to present resilience

Evidence checked 2026-11-09 · Source check date: 2026-11-09

40-point research coverage · Sources

CSU Pueblo’s archive identifies a collection of photographs, newspaper material and printed records about the 1921 flood, including material dated 1921, 1956 and 1971. The collection is accessible, but its record warns that not all material is in the public domain. Historical damage evidence does not establish today’s parcel-level flood risk.

Pueblo's memory

The 1921 flood remains part of Pueblo's history. CSU Pueblo's archive identifies photographs, newspaper material and printed records, including material dated 1921, 1956 and 1971.

Why history matters

These materials help people understand destruction, recovery and changes in the region over time. Photographs and later recollections have different dates and contexts; they need individual identification before drawing detailed conclusions.

Learning from the past

Historic experiences can inform preparedness but do not determine today's flood zone or engineering capacity. Explore the archive below and check item-specific reproduction rights.
