AI in Education

Explore what schools, colleges and learning systems are doing, what the research supports, and what families and educators should ask.

The answer

Pueblo has documented educational AI initiatives and guidelines. That does not establish district-wide implementation, independent learning gains, actual local AI spending or effective operational protections. Source review: November 18, 2026.

What the evidence shows

Pueblo School District 60

Its published guidance emphasizes staff and student literacy, privacy, academic honesty, human judgment and evaluation. District guidance

Pueblo library district

The district describes its BoodleBox collaboration and staff-first training approach. Wider access and educational outcomes need verification. Library initiative

Colorado Department of Education

Provides AI competency guidance for learners and educational professionals, emphasizing the educator's role. State guidance and resources

Does AI improve learning?

Two studies demonstrate why assisted performance and independent learning must be distinguished.

High-school mathematics — risk of overdependence

Bastani and colleagues (2025) found that unrestricted AI help improved practice performance but could harm subsequent unaided performance. Tutoring safeguards substantially reduced that harm. The study does not establish the effect in Pueblo. Original study.

College physics — structured tutoring

Kestin and colleagues (2025) found that a deliberately designed AI tutor improved measured learning in its specific randomized classroom experiment. This is not proof of long-term gains across subjects or ages. Original study.

Q's suggested approach: attempt the task, use permitted hints, verify the result, and practice without assistance. Choosing non-AI learning is valid.

Questions for parents, boards and educators

Authority and purpose

Who is responsible, what task is the tool approved for, and what happens if it fails?

Privacy and minors

What is sent to the provider? What is retained, shared or deleted? What rules apply to age and school records?

Educational results

Can learners repeat and transfer a skill without AI? What comparison group or baseline exists?

Costs

What are the license, training, review, support, infrastructure and exit costs? What was actually paid?

Fairness and safety

Are accessible non-AI options, human review and correction available?

Continuity

Can teachers provide instruction and support when the service is unavailable?

What remains unknown

School-level approved tools and use, actual contracts and AI expenditures, staff training uptake, outcomes, access gaps, privacy audits and wider comparative research remain open.
