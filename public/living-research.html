Research accountability

Available evidence, material questions, source freshness, verified claim coverage, entity relationships and release receipts are distinct measures. The 40-perspective analytical method is a research capability, not a quota of empty evidence slots. No aggregate current coverage percentage is established. The previously reported 107 bounded amendments are historical work records, not proof of 107 independently verified cases. Missing material evidence should remain visible in the individual report.
