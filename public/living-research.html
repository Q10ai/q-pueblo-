Budgets, debt and essential services

Golf course debt, public assets and water costs

County 2024 audited statements show $238,560 operating income but a $1,667,877 negative net position. Pueblo West’s 2025 audit lists $7,613,306 in long-term notes plus a $457,000 land option. Public records are spread across two institutions; no freely transferable balance is established. [S1,S2]

County budget explanation and revenue reconciliation

Commissioner-shared graphics describe capital commitments, declining marijuana revenue and rising service costs. Recalculation shows $19.068M lower proposed revenue/sources versus the 2026 budget, with lower grants and zero proposed financing accounting for $17.928M of that net change. A proposed $1.265M surplus is a narrow margin, not a final result.

County audit timing and accounting support costs

Reported financial management support had a $170,950 ceiling; it is not the independent audit fee.

County audited fund balances and proposed fiscal outlook

2024 audited General Fund balance: $41.95M, including $4.85M unassigned; proposed 2027 balance depends on assumptions.

County pension funding and workforce obligations

2024 ACFR reports $145.10M county plan net pension liability and $4.215M actuarial contribution shortfall; long-term obligations are not current bills.
