How can families apply for District 60 preschool in 2026–27?

Evidence checked 2026-11-14 · Check the listed review date.

Findings and more detail · Sources

Short answer

District 60's 2026–27 guide directs four-year-old families to complete Colorado Universal Preschool and District 60 enrollment, then wait for placement. Since April 1, enrollment is direct through the district when seats exist. Call Early Childhood at 719-253-6016. Three-year-old programs have separate qualifying criteria.

The full story

Getting started

District 60's 2026–27 guide describes state Universal Preschool and district enrollment as separate steps. After April 1, the district says direct enrollment is available where seats exist. Four-year-old eligibility uses an October 1 cutoff; three-year-old programs have separate qualifying criteria.

What families need to know

The posted morning sessions run from 8:00 to 11:45, Monday through Thursday. The posted afternoon sessions run from 12:45 to 3:15 on those days: ten clock hours, although the page labels them fifteen. This could be a posting error or incomplete schedule, not proof of missed instruction.

Practical help

Call District 60 Early Childhood at 719-253-6016 to confirm seat availability and actual hours before planning work or transportation. The official application pages below explain enrollment.

What Q finds

The separate state and district steps are useful to families; the schedule mismatch matters for work and transportation planning. Ask the district to confirm hours before committing to a schedule.

What the available evidence cannot establish

  • The afternoon discrepancy may be a posting error or an incomplete schedule; it does not prove missing instructional hours, entitlement violations or wrongdoing.
  • Current seat availability, exact hours at a particular site and personal eligibility are not verified.
  • The cause of the afternoon schedule discrepancy and actual delivered instruction hours are unknown.
  • 2027–28 enrollment rules have not been reviewed.
