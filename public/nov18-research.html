Twin community observations

Supplied discussion captures include questions about audit findings, staffing, legal costs, asset sales, inflation, TIF risk and neighborhood improvements. Support and disagreement are both retained as signals in the controlled intake. Comments are evidence of expressed questions or experiences, not proof that underlying allegations are true. Raw screenshots, names, political profiles and sensitive employee details are not published here. No live Twin import established.

Signal processing: preserve observation, deduplicate overlapping captures, classify claim separately, link existing issues, verify independently, propose LL/RX, and retain corrections. Historical signal observations and current claim freshness have different lifecycles.

Proposed review: daily before imminent budget/ordinance decisions; on new official records and quarterly for TIF/corridor. No automated monitoring enabled.
