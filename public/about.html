About Q-Pueblo

Q-Pueblo is an independent community research beta. It is not a government office, legal adviser, accredited audit organization, emergency service or live AI chatbot. It uses dated public records to explain questions, choices and consequences, while people retain responsibility for decisions.

Public information is free to read. No purchase or account is required for the current static research tools.

Who publishes and funds it?

The public code is maintained in the Q10ai Q-Pueblo repository. The formal legal operator identity, complete funding disclosures, staffing, sponsor arrangements and conflicts register have not yet been independently established and published. Do not infer them from the repository name. The site does not claim certification or governmental endorsement.

How conclusions are controlled

Original sources, dates, assumptions and uncertainties should stay visible. The editorial method requires human review before publishing consequential conclusions. The existence of a review requirement is not proof of an independently staffed review service. A sponsor or contributor should not buy a favorable finding; formal funding and conflict disclosures remain open.
