About Q-Pueblo

Know Pueblo. Understand what shapes our community. Help shape its future.

Q-Pueblo is the Pueblo-focused civic project within Q. It helps residents, community leaders, businesses, educators and public officials understand local decisions, follow public money, explore issues and compare possible solutions.

Our purpose is to help people make better-informed decisions, not make decisions for them. Q-Pueblo is independent of government. Public research is free to read.

Independence, ownership and funding

Q-Pueblo is not a government agency. Formal legal operator identity, complete funding disclosures, staffing, sponsor arrangements and a conflicts register have not yet been independently established and published. No government endorsement or certification is claimed.

Research standards and service limits

Findings should distinguish evidence, assumptions and uncertainty. Consequential new conclusions require human review; this does not establish an independently staffed review service. Sponsorship must not determine findings. Ask Q searches published material rather than providing a live AI conversation. Q-Pueblo is not an emergency, legal or accredited audit service and does not offer autonomous research, payments or access to confidential archives through this site.
