About Q-Pueblo

Q-Pueblo is an independent civic governance intelligence project. It serves everyone in the regional system, from an individual resident to elected officials, public employees, community leaders, educators, businesses and institutions. Its purpose is to improve understanding, self-governance and decision-making through evidence about public decisions, resources, authority, consequences and alternatives. The same evidence standards apply regardless of influence or position. It is not a government office, legal adviser, accredited audit organization, emergency service or live AI chatbot. It uses dated public records to explain questions, choices and consequences, while people retain responsibility for decisions.

Public information is free to read. No purchase or account is required for the current static research tools.

Who is behind Q-Pueblo?

Q-Pueblo is an independent civic governance intelligence project, not a government agency. Explore governance findings. No account or purchase is needed to read its public research.

How conclusions are controlled

Original sources, dates, assumptions and uncertainties should stay visible. The editorial method requires human review before publishing consequential conclusions. The existence of a review requirement is not proof of an independently staffed review service. A sponsor or contributor should not buy a favorable finding; formal funding and conflict disclosures remain open.
