How Q-Pueblo organizes the information

Topic · find a subject

Water, housing, public money and other areas for navigation. Topics do not prove an issue exists or is current.

Issue · open a real record

A stable ID, title, jurisdiction, date, source leads and research status. Multiple topics can connect to one issue as the graph develops, without double-counting.

Report · use the work

Supported findings, counterevidence, money-stage analysis, alternatives, child impact and useful next actions when evidence is sufficient.

Update · show what changed

New meeting, document or community signal enters a human-reviewed correction queue before it changes a published conclusion.

Looking for something missing? Open the coverage reconciliation queue or the research workbench. A missing issue does not become a new counted issue until an independent ID, relationship and evidence review is completed.
