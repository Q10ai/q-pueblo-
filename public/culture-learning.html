Learn-to-Learn: how to build a reliable answer

Start with a question. Identify who reported it, what was measured, the date, scope and financial or decision stage. Compare original records and different explanations. Try a scenario where useful, then revise the conclusion if newer evidence changes it. A source gap is a valid result, not an invitation to invent a number.

Example: the Leonardo proposal changed from $1,817,044 to $885,000, a $932,044 difference, before the September rejection. Learn to connect the agenda, budget, lease, nonprofit filing and observed outcomes rather than treating repeated commentary as independent evidence.
