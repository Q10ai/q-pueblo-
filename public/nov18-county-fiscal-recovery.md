# County fiscal recovery: what the 2027 budget really means

Dated 2026-11-18. Living public research edition; no independent audit or live monitoring. A proposed $9.81 million reduction is not an audited insolvency finding. Reconcile unrestricted cash, service floors and recurring savings before irreversible decisions.

## 1. Answer now
The October proposal targets $9.81m of General Fund reductions; this is not proof of either imminent shutdown or fiscal recovery. [B,D]

## 2. Decision
Compare adoption, lawful amendments and recurring alternatives at the budget hearing; verify the latest calendar. [B]

## 3. History
The county has described weaker marijuana receipts and rising insurance, medical and debt expenses. [B,L]

## 4. Current position
The October proposal is dated; actual November unrestricted cash is not established by a budget document. [B]

## 5. Dates
October 15 proposal; December 10 adoption hearing reported. Reconfirm before attending. [B,L]

## 6. People
Employees, vendors, taxpayers and service users face distinct risks; no specific layoff count is verified.

## 7. Children
Test impacts on food, housing, child support and emergency services; retain aggregate data only.

## 8. Households
Refer affected workers to state labor and county benefit resources; eligibility is individual. [S]

## 9. Region
Roads, jail, municipal services and county finances are separate accounts with interdependencies.

## 10. Fairness
Compare each office’s eligible base, exemptions and service constraints, not a uniform headline percentage.

## 11. Authority
Commissioners appropriate and elected offices choose lawful operational reductions; county dashboard describes this division. [D]

## 12. Decision owners
Finance and Treasurer reconcile cash; each elected office identifies service risks; legal counsel reviews authority.

## 13. Rules
Fund restrictions and debt covenants limit transfers; verify the governing instruments before counting savings.

## 14. Commitments
County reports a 10% reserve minimum and 16.67% target; this is recovery policy, not current cash. [D]

## 15. Oversight
Require independent scoped reconciliation, response from affected offices and a dated corrective-action ledger. [O]

## 16. Revenue
The ledger reports proposed General Fund revenue about $122.7m; forecasts are not collections. [L]

## 17. Costs
Proposed spending after reductions about $121.4m, annual surplus about $1.26m. Distinguish recurring from one-time. [L]

## 18. Approved versus proposed
2027 proposal not final; never describe projected cuts as implemented. [B]

## 19. Paid
Actual 2026 spend and payroll cash require ledger and bank reconciliation.

## 20. Value
Measure timely statutory services per net dollar, not only lower appropriations.

## 21. System
15% applies to eligible General Fund personnel costs, not every department’s total; Human Services Fund not cut under this proposal. [D]

## 22. Capacity
Sheriff’s proposed reduction about $4.9m. Safe patrol and detention staffing is not established by that number. [L]

## 23. Outcomes
Track actual savings, overtime, response times, waitlists and missed obligations.

## 24. Maintenance
Track deferred roads, facilities, IT maintenance and resulting lifecycle liabilities separately.

## 25. Obstacles
Asset sales and financing are one-time sources, potentially delayed; confirm actual closing and restrictions.

## 26. Crosswalk
Jail costs, workforce reductions, city jail demand and TIF distribution each need separate records.

## 27. EE
Deduplicate cost categories; expense reimbursements are not net regional savings.

## 28. Environment
Avoid deferring safety-critical drainage and asset maintenance without lifecycle review.

## 29. Stress
At a $1.26m proposed surplus, a $2m adverse variance would produce roughly a $0.74m deficit if all else unchanged; not a cash forecast.

## 30. T2Y
Monitor reserve rebuilding, debt maturities, vacancies, turnover and service deterioration over multiple years.

## 31. Options
Compare vacancy savings, procurement, shared services and legally available revenues against service floors and timing.

## 32. Tradeoffs
Asset sales bridge cash once; recurring gaps need recurring solutions. Short-term cuts may increase later costs.

## 33. LL
GFOA cash forecasting offers a practical template; build a rolling 13-week cash schedule. [G]

## 34. RX
Publish reconciled cash forecast, change log, exemptions and department-level net savings with protected records withheld.

## 35. Help
Use the official budget portal and attend confirmed hearings; benefit and emergency assistance through Human Services. [B,S]

## 36. Evidence strength
Official proposal and dashboard support the reduction target; the secondary budget comparison supports rounded estimates.

## 37. Contrary evidence
The county reports accounting assistance, finance hires and control reforms; assess outcomes rather than presume no action. [D]

## 38. Unknowns
Exact payroll liquidity, ledger-level causes, full 2025 audit documents, exemptions and real recurring deficit.

## 39. Reopen
A revised budget, audit, cash statement or completed financing changes the analysis; check before decisions.

## 40. Qified synthesis
A service-constrained fiscal recovery must prove net savings, cash timing and accountable outcomes simultaneously.
