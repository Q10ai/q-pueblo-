Full story and context

The current County and Southern Colorado Building Department pages distinguish City of Pueblo/Town of Boone projects from unincorporated Pueblo County projects. A Pueblo mailing address alone does not identify the permitting authority.

What changed

Local building-permit guidance distinguishes the City of Pueblo and Town of Boone from unincorporated Pueblo County. Projects in the City or Boone use the Southern Colorado Building Department; unincorporated areas, including Pueblo West, use the County Building Division, subject to parcel confirmation.

Why it matters

A Pueblo mailing address does not tell you which authority handles your project. Applying to the appropriate office helps prevent avoidable delays, and maintaining access to historical permits matters when properties change hands.

How to use it

Confirm the parcel's jurisdiction and the permit type before applying. The official portals below provide current application guidance and historical records; project-specific fees and approvals are not calculated here.
