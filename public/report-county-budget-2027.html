Short answer

The posted County presentation compares a $255.83 million 2026 budget with a $217.28 million 2027 proposal before recommended General Fund reductions: a $38.55 million difference, about 15.1%. This is a budget comparison, not $38.55 million of achieved savings or a final adopted plan.

Full story and context

The posted County presentation compares a $255.83 million 2026 budget with a $217.28 million 2027 proposal before recommended General Fund reductions: a $38.55 million difference, about 15.1%. This is a budget comparison, not $38.55 million of achieved savings or a final adopted plan.

Why the County budget matters

The preliminary presentation compares a $255.83 million 2026 budget with a $217.28 million 2027 proposal, before recommended General Fund reductions. The $38.55 million difference is about 15.1%, not savings already achieved.

Where the changes are

General Fund sources are projected to decline from $141.73 million to $122.67 million. Road and Bridge compares $22.34 million with $19.34 million, and Human Services $55.71 million with $54.79 million. The presentation attributes much of the decline in other funds to capital projects.

What it could mean

Lower planned spending could reflect completed projects, fewer one-time resources or changes to services. Families, employees and contractors may experience effects that the budget alone does not reveal. The November 14 directory check did not show a linked adopted 2027 document; actual results and the final decision remain separate.
