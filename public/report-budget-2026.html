Full story and context

The City adopted a $128,202,143 General Fund appropriation for fiscal 2026 under Ordinance 11075. This is an adopted spending authorization, not evidence that every appropriated dollar has been paid.

What happened

In November 2025 the City adopted its spending plan for 2026. Ordinance 11075 authorizes $128.2 million from the General Fund. Police account for $43.66 million and fire for $29.88 million, together roughly 57.4% of the fund. The remaining appropriations total about $54.66 million.

Why it matters

Public safety is a major part of the City's operating plan. A change to either department could affect employees, service coverage and the money available for other municipal needs. An appropriation is permission to spend, not a receipt for work delivered.

Where things stand

Finance separately lists a proposed 2027 budget. The 2026 authorization remains useful as a baseline, but actual payments, service results and the final 2027 plan are not reconciled in this study. Comparing those stages is more informative than comparing isolated budget headlines.
