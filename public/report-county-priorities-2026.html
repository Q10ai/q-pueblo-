Short answer

Pueblo County's Board of County Commissioners Smart Priorities Dashboard displays 26 initiatives: 1 complete, 22 underway, and 3 waiting, as of its August 8, 2026 update. These are County-reported statuses, not audited outcomes, payments, or live project states.

The full story

The County's plan

In its August 8 dashboard, Pueblo County grouped 26 initiatives into eight portfolios. It marked 22 underway, three waiting and one complete. The completed item concerned housing trust project checks, not a verified count of occupied homes.

Where progress matters

Financial reporting, the 2025 audit, a 2026 budget amendment and a grant-management system were marked underway. Debris removal, a roads and bridges dashboard and the detention-center solar agreement were marked waiting.

What this tells us

These priorities are connected: financial information supports decisions on roads, safety, housing and services. The August labels are the County's own dated status reports; this research does not establish their present completion or delivery outcomes.

  • A progress label may lag actual activity; the County describes its dashboard as updated monthly, but the displayed date remains August 8. Complete does not prove final spend or delivered outcomes.
  • Whether a later Board action or operational change altered any August status.
  • Contracts, exact funding, cash payments, performance outcomes and individual legal owners for each program.
  • Whether any County-listed relationship has enforceable terms; the dashboard alone cannot establish that.
