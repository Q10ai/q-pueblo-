Knowledge grows in stages.

Research doesn't have to be complete to be discoverable. An indexed subject is a starting point, not a claim that all questions have been answered. We preserve original sources, disagreements, corrections and past/present/future child impact as investigation advances.

Research maturity is not publication permission

Each object has a maturity stage and separate evidence/privacy/release status. A topic name may be public while a sensitive claim or incomplete case file is held privately.

  1. DiscoveredSignal received; authenticity and relevance not yet checked
  2. IndexedTopic or institution located in the public navigation; not a finding
  3. ResearchingSources and questions being assembled; conclusions withheld
  4. Evidence DevelopingIndividual claims checked, counterevidence sought
  5. Reviewed FindingsSpecific findings passed source and human review
  6. Comprehensive PacketFull QIFY packet with child, money, history and risk analysis
  7. MonitoringReviewed evidence monitored for dated change and TTL
  8. Superseded ArchivedOlder version retained with amendment and successor links

How a packet matures

A new source may change the current explanation, reveal a contradiction or produce a new scenario. Its original version remains connected to the amendment. No automated model, popular claim, or number of reposts can promote an allegation into fact.

Child Core protection, privacy controls, human accountability and the right to correct or challenge information remain active at every stage.

The larger historical Q-Pueblo private research inventory is not automatically imported into this public register. Public-safe metadata will be added after source/identity/privacy screening.
