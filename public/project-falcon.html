Q PUEBLO | 2026–2027 CIVIC DECISION TIMELINE

Project Falcon

What happened, what is at stake, and what must still be established. Public-source edition assembled November 15, 2026 from dated records and public reporting; not an independent audit or official government statement.

One-minute answer

Public reporting says the City approved a land option in October after the June pause. A purchase, final incentives, factory construction, delivered jobs and operating outcomes are not established by that action.

Proposal ≠ Approval · Forecast ≠ Result · Human review required.

What is at stake?

Jobs and wages

Reported projections are not delivered employment. Compare written commitments, hiring, wages and retention.

Incentives and taxpayers

The original $65 million proposal is not proof of an approved or paid package. Revised terms remain to be established.

Land and infrastructure

An option preserves a possible purchase. Compare the executed land instrument, surveyed acreage, road design, funding and asset ownership.

What should happen next?

Retrieve the signed option, county and city agreements, current incentive terms, design scopes, water and wastewater capacity evidence, and project-level expenditure records. Compare phased, full-build and no-build outcomes without assigning all shared road costs to one company.
