What a complete QIFY paper would add

  1. Short summary plus full supported research question and historical chronology.
  2. Source ledger with dates, authority, original records, contradictions, correction receipt and claim status.
  3. Entity/legal/financial/public-resource crosswalk, including upstream/downstream impacts and third-level entities.
  4. EE, EH, T2Y, Q-RX, SUPRA, GLEAN, LL and LEAP analysis as applicable; numeric models only when inputs are defensible.
  5. Counterarguments, sensitivity tests, alternatives, residual risk, remaining gaps and a human sign-off/release decision.
