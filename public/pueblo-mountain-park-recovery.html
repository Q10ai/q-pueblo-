Pueblo Mountain Park: wildfire recovery, FEMA, insurance and rebuilding

The City reports the park remains closed and FEMA documentation is underway. Two October meetings addressed recovery. CPR reports an expected insurance range of $4.9–6.3 million; cumulative receipts, project award, total costs and reopening date are not verified.
