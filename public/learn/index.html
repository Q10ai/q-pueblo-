THE Q METHOD

Learning includes the right to question Q.

01

History of histories

What happened? What was reported then? What changed when new evidence arrived? Earlier versions remain part of the story.

02

Follow the money and entities

Separate promises, approvals, obligations, transactions and outcomes. Explain who pays, benefits, decides, and bears indirect costs.

03

Defense, not exploitation

Recognize harmful techniques and practice safe responses. Advanced exercises require isolated, authorized environments.

04

Children and the future

Ask how each proposal affects children and vulnerable people across generations. Risk and uncertainty are explicit.

Evidence boundary: This is a candidate educational experience, not an official City or County program, legal advice, cybersecurity certification, or proof that Q10's internal control engines are deployed. Fictional questions are labeled. Website publication requires separate approval.
