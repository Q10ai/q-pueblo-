What Pueblo County’s published numbers already reveal about the budget crisis

Findings, evidence and Q processing

The 2024 audited General Fund balance was $41.95M, including only $4.85M unassigned. The 2027 proposal projects a narrow $1.26M annual surplus and its opening-balance assumptions include $22M of anticipated financing and asset sales. [S1,S2]

Pueblo County pension funding, workforce cuts and future obligations

Findings, evidence and Q processing

The 2024 ACFR reports $145.10M net liability for the county officers and employees plan, $423,031 PERA net liability and a $4.215M actuarial contribution shortfall for the county plan. These are long-term obligations, not current General Fund bills. [S1]
