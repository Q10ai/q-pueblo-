Q-RX: From Concern to Useful Options

A non-authoritative, source-led way to understand a conflict or failure, identify what can be verified, compare possible remedies, and give people something usable.

Q-RX is an advisory problem-solving metaphor, not a medical diagnosis or legal verdict. We can validate someone's need to be heard without declaring unsupported blame. No public body, human professional or eligible person loses authority to an AI model.
💬I need to be heardUse the private reflection worksheet and separate feeling from checkable claims.🔎Help me understandRead real source-linked records with dates and definitions.🔗Who is connected?See evidence-based association chains, while independently checking actual authority.📑What is being researched?Compare the case lead, missing evidence and current source state.📝I want a correctionUse the existing human-reviewed public evidence input route.📦Give me something usableDownload a neutral, printable evidence and options worksheet.

Understand · Diagnose · Respond · Verify

  1. Listen: record the issue in the person’s words, separating emotion and lived experience from disputed causal statements.
  2. Locate the actual system: geography, dates, laws, money state, contracts, responsible decision-makers, operations and affected people.
  3. Check competing claims: independent sources, missing records, bias, contrary evidence, rights and different legitimate moral values.
  4. Find alternatives: clarification, records request, policy reform, bounded pilot, corrective service, independent review, formal appeal, no action, or further observation.
  5. Assess cost and risk: budget, resource use, human impact, safety, fairness, maintenance, reversibility and legal constraints.
  6. Package an option: evidence card, contact map, financial timeline, comparison table, educational aid, research packet or human-reviewed intervention plan.
  7. Monitor outcomes: what did the authorized people actually do, what happened, what should change?
Never use Q-RX as accusation scoring. It cannot infer wrongdoing, intent, diagnosis, eligibility, legal responsibility, or a government decision from an allegation or graph association.
