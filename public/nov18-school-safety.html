School safety and resource officers: keeping Pueblo children safe

Residents describe SROs as valued trusted adults. No approved staffing reduction has been verified. Protect safety, rights and continuity through a verified joint funding and service review.

1. Answer now

District 70 confirms a partnership with the county sheriff. No current program termination or staffing reduction has been verified. [1]

2. Decision

Review the executed service agreement, net annual cost and continuity before changing SRO services.

3. History

The sheriff's 2024 report describes education, mentoring and law enforcement functions in schools. [2]

4. Present conditions

Three supplied comments describe valued SRO services. These are community observations, not independent measurements or staffing counts.

5. Dates

Historical 2024 baseline; May 26, 2026 district FY27 adoption; November county deliberations. [2] [3] [4]

6. Affected people

Children, families, educators, administrators, officers, counselors and taxpayers.

7. Child impact

Measure safety, belonging, fear, discipline, access to trusted adults and student rights.

8. Families

Offer an official continuity briefing and clear help pathways without collecting child identifiers.

9. Geography

District 70 is not District 60; city police and county sheriff have distinct agreements and authority.

10. Equity

Evaluate disability access and disparities using protected aggregate outcome indicators.

11. Authority

District governs education; sheriff governs law enforcement assignments subject to law, funds and agreements.

12. Owners

District board and administration, sheriff, BOCC and counsel should jointly review changes.

13. Rules

Obtain the executed MOU or IGA, duties, training and discipline boundaries before making legal conclusions.

14. Commitments

District FY27 budget was adopted May 26; this alone does not confirm an SRO appropriation or renewal. [3]

15. Oversight

Publish aggregate outcomes, complaints process, changes and independent findings.

16. Revenue

Trace district contributions, county payroll funding, eligible grants and in-kind support. Actual shares unknown.

17. Cost

Include compensation, benefits, vehicles, training, administration and backfill.

18. Approval

Obtain signed agreements, board actions, renewal/termination terms and verified funding commitments.

19. Payment

Reconcile actual invoices, disbursements and receipts; appropriations are not cash.

20. Value

Measure outcomes and net resource cost, not officer count alone.

21. Process

District describes prevention, crisis planning and law enforcement roles. [1]

22. Capacity

Evaluate aggregate services and workload privately. Do not disclose school-specific vulnerabilities or tactical coverage.

23. Quality

Track aggregate assistance, incidents, referrals, complaints, student feedback and follow-up.

24. Reliability

Maintain lawful backup, training and multidisciplinary crisis planning.

25. Obstacles

Budget constraints, staffing, agreements, recruitment and grant restrictions can limit options.

26. Crosswalk

County cash and sheriff workforce influence school continuity; municipal detention and education budgets have distinct authorities.

27. EE

Avoid duplicate expenses; interagency transfers cancel only in combined regional accounts.

28. Physical conditions

Review accessibility and safety through qualified planning; protect sensitive facility information.

29. Stress

Test loss of grants, delayed reimbursements and rising expenses without publishing operational vulnerabilities.

30. T2Y

Track annual funding, retention, student rights, safety and trust with evidence revisions.

31. Options

Preserve services if feasible; examine cost-sharing, eligible grants, counseling, prevention and crisis teams.

32. Tradeoffs

National evidence finds reduced forms of violence alongside increased disciplinary actions and no prevention of gun-related incidents in one study. [5]

33. LL

Colorado offers multidisciplinary safety assistance; a two-district study found mixed student safety perceptions. [6] [7] [8]

34. RX

Joint verified cost and continuity statement, written role limits, public input and independent outcome review.

35. Help

District Student Services, Colorado school safety and Safe2Tell; call 911 for immediate danger. [1] [7] [9]

36. Evidence strength

Institutional descriptions, historical report and research. PDF text retrieved; screenshot fetch failed. Not current verified staffing.

37. Contrary evidence

Positive testimony matters alongside evidence of potential discipline harms; neither proves present local effects. [5] [6]

38. Unknowns

Executed agreement, cost share, staffing plan, eligibility, baseline outcomes and approved changes.

39. Reopen

On revised budget, agreement, confirmed staffing action, outcome data or a correction.

40. Qified synthesis

Protect children through verified affordability, lawful roles, measurable safety and accessible alternatives.
