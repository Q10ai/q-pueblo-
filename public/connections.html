Authority and trickle-down inquiry

  1. Verify the identity and jurisdiction of every name; do not mistake an umbrella label for a single legal body.
  2. Follow actual legal authority: voter provision, adopted rule, governing board, delegated officer, signed agreement, permit or court record.
  3. Follow real flows: funding authorization versus payment, ownership versus operation, supplier versus client, regulator versus regulated entity.
  4. Measure impact: workers, children, residents, local resources, ecosystems, upstream constraints and future liabilities—with denominators and time horizons.
  5. Test contradictory explanations: a shared label is not proof of an agenda, conflict of interest, negligence, crime or causation.
  6. Provide an option: request a record, ask a verifiable question, compare alternatives, or document a bounded RX response.
