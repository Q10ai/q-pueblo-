Where can Pueblo households get help with food?

Pueblo County lists emergency food assistance at no cost for qualifying households. Text FOOD to 1-877-644-3663 to receive an intake survey link. Seniors age 60 or older may also qualify for Everyday Eats; SNAP is a separate application and EBT benefit.

How can families apply for District 60 preschool in 2026–27?

District 60's 2026–27 guide directs four-year-old families to complete Colorado Universal Preschool and District 60 enrollment, then wait for placement. Since April 1, enrollment is direct through the district when seats exist. Call Early Childhood at 719-253-6016. Three-year-old programs have separate qualifying criteria.

November 2026 Pueblo County election: counting and certification

Pueblo County has posted the November 13 unofficial results update: 69,917 voters out of 116,192 (60.17%). Selected local counts: 2A had 14,044 yes and 24,683 no; 2B had 23,671 yes and 15,392 no; library 6A had 39,173 yes and 26,654 no. Earlier returns remain historical snapshots, not final results.

What it means: The latest published update is unofficial. More yes or no votes in this report is not a certified result or legal implementation date. Districtwide contests beyond Pueblo County require results from all applicable jurisdictions. Do not transfer officeholder authority without verifying the effective term.

County's published timeline lists November 24 for risk-limiting audit reporting and November 25 for canvass/official abstract; verify any changes. Certification and taking office are separate events.
