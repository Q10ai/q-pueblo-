Start with facts

The City lists more than 70 park sites, over 682 acres and two municipal golf courses, Elmwood and Walking Stick. These figures describe facilities, not water use. Verify actual irrigation water source, seasonal meter records, conversion rights and operator for each park, course and district; do not assume potable water. Separate recreation and heat-relief value from private profits.

Water rights and agriculture

Assess lawful diversion, type of use, priority, yield under drought, return-flow duty and local food/economic value. Paper rights and water supply are not interchangeable. Use the basin diagram as navigation, not as a legal opinion. Evaluate farm, municipal, and industrial projects on their individual instruments and measured outcomes.

Major business and AI infrastructure

Require a site-specific water-service commitment, source, rights, annual and peak demand, consumptive use, return flows, cooling technology, energy, wastewater, drought curtailment and who pays for expansion. Dry or hybrid cooling may trade water for power or capital. Separate project Falcon, Ecoasis, Comanche transition and any data-center proposals; no claim that any has a particular cooling design or water allocation.

Q public-value gate

Before supporting an investment, ask whether firm supply, resilient grid, rates, emergency services, jobs, subsidy, clawback, exit and environmental consequences are independently established. Compare feasible alternatives with lifecycle cost. The industrial calculator models stated annual direct-site water intensity only; it does not estimate power-generation water or certify available supply.

2027/2029/2031

2027 obtain sector-level water ledger; 2029 compare actual consumption, public benefits, rate burden and drought impact; 2031 reconcile rights and long-term capital plans. Protect accessible parks and household essentials; no water allocations or closures are made by Q.
