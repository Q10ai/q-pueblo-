Full story and context

Pueblo Water’s finance page lists a 2026 budget and a 2025 annual financial report. The latter includes an independent audit opinion on specified 2025/2024 financial statements. Neither document alone determines a household’s current bill, affordability, future rate or system reliability.

How the water system is financed

Pueblo Water publishes annual budgets and historical financial statements. Its finance page lists a 2026 budget and a 2025 annual report with an independent financial-statement audit opinion for the specified periods.

What that means at home

A budget describes planned resources, an audit addresses historical financial reporting, and a household bill depends on the applicable rates and actual use. A favorable financial opinion is not a guarantee of affordability or system reliability.

What would make the picture complete

The available documents are useful starting evidence. This study has not reconciled current tariffs, representative household bills or service outcomes. Use the official financial hub below for the documents and the applicable provider's tariff for a specific bill.
