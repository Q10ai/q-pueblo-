City of Pueblo 2026 property tax mill levy — Ordinance 11074

Evidence checked 2026-11-09 · Source check date: 2026-11-09

40-point research coverage · Sources

City of Pueblo Ordinance 11074 establishes a 15.633-mill City property-tax levy for the 2026 budget year. It is the City's rate only, not a total bill that also reflects County, school, district and other levies.

How local property tax works

A mill represents $1 of tax per $1,000 of taxable assessed value. Pueblo adopted a City levy of 15.633 mills for 2026. For $10,000 of taxable assessed value, that would be $156.33 for the City component alone, before other applicable adjustments.

What the records reveal

The supporting material estimates $21,280,697 in property-tax revenue, but its fiscal-year descriptions conflict: the background identifies 2026 while the ordinance clause labels that amount 2024. The rate is documented separately from the disputed-year estimate.

What it means for a homeowner

A home's market value is not its taxable assessed value. County, school and other district levies are separate, so the City rate cannot determine the full bill. Actual collections and the source discrepancy remain unresolved.

    Related evidence and context
