Quick view → two-minute paper → full packet

Start with a short explanation, then read the evidence and detailed analysis. Existing subject identities and earlier editions carry forward. An edition's completion depends on its attached evidence and review, not its title.

The public full-Q answer contract calls for:

  1. Question, geography, jurisdiction and valid time.
  2. Current facts, claims, estimates and unknowns separated.
  3. Primary sources, provenance, dates and contradictions.
  4. Actors and actual authority roles.
  5. Money/resource chain and financial state.
  6. TTL, T2Y, EE and EH assessment, with scope and evidence.
  7. Child, privacy and sensitive-person impacts.
  8. Dependencies, security, rollback and external constraints.
  9. Multiple solution candidates, RX paths and bounded LEAP experiments.
  10. Cross-system effects and second-order risks.
  11. Lawful human decision-maker and required review.
  12. Expected outcome, measurement, reopen triggers and corrections.

Engineering, simulations, models, diagrams, worksheets and test receipts belong with the packet when they exist and apply. A planned or absent component must remain identified as such.

Source: Q10ASI full-Q answer contract. This is the public reading path; the recovered private packet outlines and source originals retain their separate review controls.
