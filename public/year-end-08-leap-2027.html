LEAP 2027: Modernizing Pueblo without breaking Pueblo

Dated research candidate · November 18, 2026

Findings, attributed reports and Q scenarios are distinct. Not an independent completed audit or a current official status. Recheck 2026-12-02; feature through December 31, then reassess for 2027. Source-level freshness controls. Historical reports remain accessible.

Key evidence and current update

Modernization does not require replacement of every legacy system. The public website remains dated and static; no operational regional AI system or live government integration is established by this research.

Article

Pueblo's modernization challenge is not to replace every old system with AI. It is to move from fragmented processes to reliable services while budgets are strained. Establish baselines, maintain legacy systems as authoritative until tested, and add modular tools only where they improve service and protect rights. City, county, school and other authorities remain distinct.

Architecture and sequence

1. Discover systems, licenses, contract expiration, data ownership, integration and manual workflows. 2. Classify records; define retention, accessibility and role-based access. 3. Standardize exports, identifiers and reconcile financial/accounting definitions. 4. Use read-only connectors, isolated testing and source citations. 5. Parallel-run against validated manual outputs. 6. Cutover only by accountable approval; retain rollback and archive. 7. Retire duplicated systems only after obligations and restoration tested.

Workforce and local ecosystem

Create training paths for clerks, finance, IT, procurement, supervisors and frontline staff. Explore community college/university, libraries and workforce partners through voluntary agreements; no partnership or funding commitment assumed. Maintain analog service access and pay staff for approved training time.

Budget and EE

Start with AI governance scenario in article 07; do not duplicate its funding. Price legacy replacement separately after inventory. Five-year TCO = contract + hosting + integration + labor + security + training + migration + exit + maintenance. Compare doing nothing, optimizing existing systems, shared approved services and competitive replacement. Avoid heavy local compute absent demonstrated need and energy/cooling/maintenance budget.

Q twin / shadow / data contract

A read-only city/county twin holds aggregate fiscal, service and infrastructure measures with distinct jurisdictions, time stamps, units and provenance. Shadow model compares possible unintended effects; it is not a person-level profile or real-time official operational system. Reuse canonical issue IDs; link current articles through editorial aliases; counts derived dynamically from unique active records.

Child and equity

Report wait times, accessibility, language and non-digital service availability, child/family resource continuity. No individual children, health, benefits or criminal data in public twin. Ensure staff can explain and reverse errors.

T2Y milestones and acceptance

Dec 2026 inventory mandate and 2027 funding route; Q1 data contracts, training and non-sensitive pilot; Q2 independent tests and manual comparison; Q3 staged read-only integrations; Q4 publish performance and 2028 recommendations. 2028 expand only proven improvements. Criteria: reconciled outputs, source provenance, restored backups, accessibility, employee support, measured lifecycle cost and security review.

Event horizons / Rx

Halt on unauthorized disclosure, unsafe automation, unexplained reconciliation gap, loss of human access, inability to export or restore, or budget cap breach. Use rollback and manual fallback; no mass legacy replacement, layoffs based solely on projected productivity, or automatic production activation.

Next action

Identify departmental owners, public engagement, inventories and approved budget sources. Publish a roadmap with options and measured outputs, link fiscal recovery and public safety reports. Recheck at procurements, budget decisions and material system changes.

Q processing and scenario

Five-year total cost = implementation + contracts + staffing + hosting + energy + security + training + migration + exit. Compare optimize-existing, shared services, staged replacement and no-build baselines.

Red-team and release gates

Exercise backup restore, reconciliation, vendor exit, accessibility and manual continuity; stop on unexplained errors, budget breach or privacy failure.

Tools and builds — actual versus proposed

Proposed modular integration fabric: source register → data contracts → read-only connector → parallel reconciliation → human gate → monitored cutover → rollback and archival. A sandbox is not production authority.

Existing Q workbench · Records planner. Proposed modules require data, funding, testing and authorization.

What you can do next

Invite IT, finance, legal, frontline staff, community colleges and residents to define a bounded pilot and metrics; no participation or funding assumed.
