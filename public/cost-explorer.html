How to interpret the result

Account for the missing values

Zero is treated as no input, not proof of no cost. Actual rates or bids may differ.

Keep stages separate

Approved public funding, cash paid, debt service, operations and verified benefits are different financial objects.

Understand lifecycle exposure

A large upfront cost may be only one component of a project with decades of staffing, maintenance and replacement obligations.
