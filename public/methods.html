Living civic research · accountable human control

Q-Pueblo does the work it can defend.

Q is a continuously developed civic research and learning process—not an official authority, autonomous decision maker or machine that claims perfect knowledge. It organizes original public materials into findings, unresolved questions, competing explanations, useful actions and reversible corrections.

How AI is used: AI may suggest explanations and areas to investigate. An AI answer, model output, news article or public observation is not itself proof. Claim-level source provenance, dates, uncertainty and human review determine what can be published as a supported finding.

Traceable process

Gather → Verify → Analyze → Report → Improve

01

Gather with provenance

Collect the original record, its publisher, valid date, jurisdiction and precise statement. Make community observations available as leads, not facts.

02

Challenge and compare

Crosswalk direct and secondary roles, legal authority, money lifecycle and strongest counterevidence; no guilt by association or fabricated missing records.

03

Publish and amend

Deliver explanation, practical options and source citations; include a child-impact assessment. Preserve historical versions, error corrections and human review receipts.

Past · Present · Future

Child-impact lens

How do decisions affect children's schooling, health, mobility, costs and future obligations? Aggregated effects only: no profiling or indexing of identifiable children.

Open critical reasoning

Transparency includes disagreement

Power, budgets, jobs and public trust create competing incentives. Q records contrary evidence and a right to correct without asserting that dissent proves misconduct or bad faith.

SHOCK · LEAP · RX

Prepare, don't pretend to predict.

Compare disruption scenarios and recovery pathways under explicit assumptions. Backup sources and safe fallbacks matter more than unsupported AI certainty. Digital-twin and quantum-inspired systems remain design-stage.

Corrections and participation

Open a source-aware draft in Contribute & Challenge. The current form makes a local record and does not send it to us; publication of submitted facts requires a separate secure, human-reviewed service. Q's issue index and source directories are not a public evidence certification.

Reports are versioned, cited and labeled. A proposed expenditure, authorization, contract, payment and realized benefit must not be mistaken for the same accounting stage or double-counted. Any consequential claim can be revised with documented counterevidence and a public as-of date.
