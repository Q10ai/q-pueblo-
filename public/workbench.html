HOW Q PUEBLO GOVERNS KNOWLEDGE

From signal to accountable explanation

Accuracy & uncertainty

Proposals, authorizations, appropriations, contracts, payments and outcomes are different. Do not merge them into one dollar amount.

RX backup to backup

A copied headline does not become an independent source. Use separate origin records, archives, alternative custodians and versioned corrections.

Child & community impact

Test direct and indirect effects, distribution, access, safety and future liabilities. Do not profile children or residents.

Reuse and integration

Use public IDs, dated metadata and source links. Automated ingestion, private records and high-stakes decisions need additional authorization and review.
