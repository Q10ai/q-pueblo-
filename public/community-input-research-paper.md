## 40 research perspectives

Qified editorial analysis; not forty independently certified answers for each underlying investigation.

### 1. Purpose

Convert concerns into source-bounded questions, not votes or findings by popularity.

### 2. Scope

Eleven historical clusters, fourteen linked collections; not exhaustive community input.

### 3. Coverage

Screenshots, prompts, media, testimony and formal agency processes differ; originals not exhaustively recovered.

### 4. Provenance

Retain event/capture date, publisher, locator and exact edition; raw material stays controlled.

### 5. Classification

Separate experience, opinion, allegation, official statement, adopted action, reporting and inference.

### 6. Deduplication

Overlaps and repeated summaries do not add independent evidence.

### 7. Representation

No representative polling denominator or authenticated contributor count exists.

### 8. Freshness

Reopen when records, decisions or corrections arrive; preserve original snapshots; editorial recheck December 2.

### 9. Jurisdiction

City, County, district, state and federal authorities differ; cooperation does not confer control.

### 10. Delivery

Trace adopted vote, assigned administration, funding and completed work via S1–S3.

### 11. Finance

Distinguish request, appropriation, commitment, cash, reimbursement and audit; eliminate duplicate transfers.

### 12. Procurement

Seek solicitation, addenda, evaluation, award, change orders and acceptance; missing records do not prove misconduct.

### 13. Half-Cent

Trace eligibility, remaining cash, restricted funds, commitments and additional economic outcomes.

### 14. Infrastructure

Split pre-existing maintenance and incremental demand; assess lifecycle cost and asset condition.

### 15. Urban renewal

Follow adopted plan, adjusted base, increment, reimbursements and public benefit. [S4]

### 16. Jobs and training

Measure filled positions, actual resident hires, wage bands, credentials and retention.

### 17. Local business

Assess permitting time, access and cost without exposing confidential firm records.

### 18. Tourism scenario

3,009,507 visits × 5% × $25 ≈ $3.76m; 15% × $40 ≈ $18.06m; 30% × $60 ≈ $54.17m illustrative gross added purchases. Excludes displacement, leakage and cost; not tax revenue or forecast.

### 19. Preservation

Compare operating costs, condition, cultural access and alternative uses; one appraisal cannot decide public value.

### 20. Housing

Track completed and occupied homes, affordability, transport and repairs; planned unit is not occupancy.

### 21. Environment

Separate hazard, possible and measured exposure, and health outcomes. No personal exposure claims.

### 22. Water and drainage

Test runoff, maintenance and capacity against engineered requirements and measurements.

### 23. Health and services

Verify actual geography, eligibility, availability and waitlists; funding announcements are not service confirmation.

### 24. Public safety

### 25. Child and family impacts

### 27. Workforce continuity

## Research paper: method, findings and limitations

**Abstract.** This longitudinal, qualitative case synthesis evaluates how public questions can generate accountable research without equating online visibility with prevalence. Eleven previously recovered clusters are compared across fourteen existing editorial collections. The observational material is non-random, incomplete and sometimes duplicated. No statistical inference about Pueblo residents is warranted.

**Research question.** Can an evidence-controlled workflow convert a concern into a specific, independently checkable proposition, an accountable action option, and an outcome measure without compromising privacy or duplicating work? The evidence supports feasibility of the workflow at a design and offline reference level; it does not establish improvements in public outcomes.

**Method.** Unit of analysis: issue-level question, not individual or social account. Recover earlier artifacts; classify source family and claim type; preserve contrary editions; compare against relevant official records; route to existing issue IDs; retain unresolved questions. Treat dates of event, publication, retrieval and legal effect separately. Qualitative clustering is editorial, not a coded random sample. Original screenshots and formal submissions were not completely recovered, so intercoder reliability, prevalence and response rates cannot be calculated.

**Result.** The recovered crosswalk joins 11 clusters to 14 collections, retaining 4 collections with no mapping in this subset. Common structural gaps concern actual payments versus authorization, adopted action versus implementation, announcement versus delivered service, and attribution versus proof. These are recurring evidence problems, not estimates of institutional misconduct.

**Counterfactual.** A single unverified claim can generate multiple duplicate reports. A source return and versioned correction can avoid that duplication. Conversely, overly aggressive matching could erase genuinely independent observations or suppress adverse evidence. Keep separate original editions, review contested mappings and allow correction or appeal.

**Limits.** No original-byte certification, full chat census, current service outcome study or population-level poll. This paper is an editorial synthesis, not a claim of causal improvement. Published records may change; future readers should verify the controlling document.

## Evaluation and implementation packet

Measure the process before claiming improvements: percentage of material propositions linked to originals, independent source-family count, percentage of changed claims with correction history, unresolved high-impact issues, time from first identified gap to verified response, and repeated record requests avoided. Set the denominator before each review. A missing denominator is unknown, not zero.

Compare three options: retain the current manual evidence review; use the existing offline deduplication and routing reference under human review; or integrate a controlled workflow only after privacy, access, review, rollback and outcome tests. No automated public verdict or live intake is proposed for activation here. Costs require actual staff time, infrastructure and maintenance records; no estimated savings are presented as realized savings.

Review triggers: new budget or payment evidence, board or Council action, source correction, revised agency decision, or a change in permission. Target December 2, 2026 for the next editorial check, not a scheduled monitoring promise. 2027 baseline and approved pilots, 2029 independent outcome comparison, 2031 longevity and discontinuation review are scenario milestones.
