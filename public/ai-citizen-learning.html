Learn at your own pace, from children and families to seniors, workers and community leaders. No AI account is needed for the practice exercises.

Public officials and professionals

Learn about approval, contracts, privacy, human review and independent outcomes.

Practice six skills

1. Recognize AI output

Distinguish a factual claim, a predicted outcome, and an opinion. A confident answer is not proof.

2. Ask a useful question

Use a fictional or public example. Ask for dates, original sources, limitations and alternative explanations.

3. Verify a claim

Find the original public document. Does its date, place and meaning match the claim?

4. Protect your information

Use fictional names and values. Never enter passwords, student records or other sensitive details.

5. Learn independently

Attempt a question unaided. Use a hint if permitted, then solve a different question without AI.

6. Decide whether to use AI

Compare the same task with and without the tool, including error and correction time.
