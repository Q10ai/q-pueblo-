Learn by doing

Children & caregivers

Discover how AI makes guesses, create stories, and spot when a computer gets something wrong.

Try this: Ask an adult to invent a question about an animal. Compare a confident answer with a library book. Draw what you learned; explain one mistake to your adult.

Teens & students

Study, investigate claims, explore careers and build independent judgment.

Try this: A video claims AI will replace every job in Pueblo by 2030. Identify the claim, evidence needed and one alternative explanation. Research two local occupations using dated sources; do not present speculation as a prediction.

Adults & families

Use AI for everyday decisions, household planning, and recognizing misleading information.

Try this: Compare two fictional household budgets. Ask AI to find savings, independently recalculate each total and check whether any suggestion would expose personal details or create a new expense.

Build six essential AI skills

Work through each scenario before opening its answer. Try the applied challenge when you are ready for a more demanding exercise. These are fictional practice scenarios, not claims about current Pueblo events.

1. Recognize AI output

Try it: An AI answer says a fictional bus route runs every 15 minutes. Is this a fact, a prediction or an unsupported claim?

Check your reasoning

Check the actual schedule. Until then it is an unverified factual claim.

Go deeper · applied challenge

Compare a forecast, an opinion and a source-backed statement. Label the source and date of each. Explain why confidence alone cannot establish truth.

2. Ask a useful question

Try it: Rewrite “Tell me about the Pueblo budget” so you get a specific, checkable answer.

Check your reasoning

Specify the year, government, funding category and original source; ask for uncertainty and distinguish proposed from adopted spending.

Go deeper · applied challenge

Design a follow-up question that tests a competing explanation, missing information and what new evidence would change your conclusion.

3. Verify a claim

Try it: A fictional social post says a city approved a $5 million project. What would you check first?

Check your reasoning

Find the relevant agenda, minutes, ordinance or contract. Distinguish proposal, approval, funding and actual payment; compare dates and amounts.

Go deeper · applied challenge

Suppose two records disagree. Establish jurisdiction, fiscal year, document version, source authority and remaining uncertainty. Write a short conclusion without hiding the conflict.

4. Protect your information

Try it: You want AI to explain a school or medical letter. What should you remove before sharing a fictionalized example?

Check your reasoning

Remove names, addresses, identifiers, account numbers and sensitive details. Prefer a made-up example; use a qualified person for consequential decisions.

Go deeper · applied challenge

Map a hypothetical app's data flow: input, vendor, retention, sharing, deletion and access. Identify information that should never leave your control.

5. Learn independently

Try it: Try explaining why a water bill can rise before using AI. Then ask for one hint and revise your explanation.

Check your reasoning

Explain the answer in your own words. Check the published rate schedule, usage and fees. Test your understanding with a different example.

Go deeper · applied challenge

Compare your unaided answer and assisted answer against the same rubric: accuracy, reasoning, sources and ability to solve a new problem without assistance.

6. Decide whether to use AI

Try it: A manual task takes 20 minutes. An AI version takes 5 minutes to generate and 18 minutes to verify and correct. Which is faster?

Check your reasoning

The manual task takes 20 minutes; the AI-assisted task takes 23 minutes. Speed alone is insufficient; compare accuracy and risk too.

Go deeper · applied challenge

Include setup, energy or resource cost where measurable, licensing, errors, supervision and exit plans. Test a small reversible use case and identify when not to automate.
