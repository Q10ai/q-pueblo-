Water shortage

What Q found: Pueblo Water publishes a 2026 budget and a 2025 annual financial report. The audit covers specified financial statements, not future water reliability or your household bill. These records alone do not establish a current water shortage.

What could help: leak repairs and conservation can reduce demand; supply investments may improve resilience but need funding and time. The benefits depend on actual water use and available supply.

Wildfire and disaster

Justice, law and public safety

Understand the full justice lifecycle and the distinct responsibilities of agencies.

Read the dated Pueblo court and detention study: 40 perspectives, evidence and next steps.

Law and jurisdiction

Distinguish ordinances, state statutes, federal law, civil rights and procedural requirements. Verify the applicable authority and current law before relying on it.

Enforcement

Separate calls, incidents, investigation, arrest and charging; an arrest is not a conviction. Examine staffing, response times, complaints, use-of-force reporting and oversight without profiling people.

Courts and legal access

Distinguish municipal, county, district and federal jurisdiction. Examine prosecution, defense, pretrial, adjudication, appeals, legal aid and barriers to access.

Jail, corrections and reentry

Compare detention capacity, operating cost, treatment, lawful alternatives, supervision, rehabilitation and reentry outcomes. Include victims, staff, families and community safety.

Child justice

Protect minors' privacy and evaluate prevention, education, family support and rehabilitation. No individual risk scoring or legal decisions by Q.

Housing, homelessness and community stability

Acknowledge competing concerns while assessing pathways, capacity and outcomes.

Read the dated housing and homelessness study, assistance routes and 40 perspectives.

Housing pressures

Compare rents, wages, vacancy, construction, eviction prevention and affordable supply. Distinguish chronic homelessness, temporary displacement, family homelessness and disaster recovery.

Community perspectives

Include people experiencing homelessness, residents, businesses, service workers, police, taxpayers and children. Separate reported experiences from population-level evidence.

RX options

Compare prevention, shelter, transitional and supportive housing, treatment, employment support and lawful public-space management. Evaluate total regional costs, privacy, service capacity and long-term stability.

Measurement

Avoid using counts of people moved from one location as proof of resolution. Measure housing retention, access, safety and verified service outcomes.

Follow the Money: public integrity

Trace funding, influence and authority without assuming misconduct.

Financial chain

Identify source, appropriation, obligation, contract, disbursement, expenditure, audit and outcome. Do not sum the same money at successive stages.

Public power

Trace votes, recorded roles, procurement, incentives, grants, campaign finance, disclosed interests and oversight using primary records.

Fairness and transparency

Compare beneficiaries, costs, access and alternatives. A financial association is not evidence of wrongdoing; allegations are not findings. Offer correction and right of reply.

Accountability

Record dates, amounts, jurisdiction, source locators, unresolved contradictions and legal authority; distinguish promised and measured outcomes.

Regional readiness, SYN Time and LEAP

Coordinate the transition from existing infrastructure to new capabilities.

Business and industrial readiness

Evaluate water, energy, workforce, housing, health, transportation, broadband, suppliers and public fiscal capacity before commitments. Compare retained jobs and actual outcomes with promises.

Legacy-to-AI transition

Inventory systems, digitize and verify records, assign stewardship, standardize interfaces, classify data, train workers, plan migration and vendor exit. No unapproved external access.

SYN Time

Link budget, grant, school, election, construction, staffing, infrastructure and technology timelines. Identify dependency conflicts and owners before commitments.

Defensive and opportunity LEAP

For each issue compare prevention, reversible pilots, adaptation and new opportunities at 1, 5, 10 and 20-plus years. Avoid a single forecast.

Child Core

Evaluate safety, housing, learning, creativity, privacy, future independence, environmental resources and deferred burdens. Protect agency for all ages; never rank children.

Rumor Mill: stories, trust and morals

Separate factual questions, legal questions, ethical disagreements and feelings.

Historical stories

Explore steelmill oral histories, the Hanging Tree tradition and flood history using archives; distinguish documented events from folklore and unresolved details. Do not present a legend as an established fact.

Current claims

Capture exact claim, date and source; compare evidence and contrary records. Classify supported, partial, contradicted, misleading, unresolved, outdated, opinion, folklore or prediction.

Why belief persists

Familiarity, identity, distrust, fear, economic insecurity and previous experiences can affect belief. Corrections may help without eliminating disagreement. Do not diagnose or profile submitters.

Is it right?

Compare rights, values, fairness, harms, incentives and institutional transparency separately from factual truth. Respect legitimate skepticism.

Constructive resolution

Use RX to compare communication, policy and service improvements. No unmoderated accusation board or confidential reporting promise.

AI safety, cybersecurity and digital protection

Practical defensive guidance for all ages and institutions.

Suspicious message or voice

Do not click unexpected links or send money. Independently call a known number, not one in the message. For a suspected fraudulent payment contact your institution promptly.

Account compromise

From a trusted device secure your email and affected accounts, change reused passwords, revoke unfamiliar sessions, enable MFA or passkeys, review recovery methods and contact the provider.

Children and families

Avoid sharing personal details, locations, school records or intimate images with unapproved tools. Use age-appropriate controls and trusted-adult support; report exploitation to appropriate authorities.

AI-specific risks

Verify important claims; treat external content as untrusted; restrict agent permissions; protect credentials; check for prompt injection, data or memory poisoning, fabricated media and overreliance.

Business and public institutions

Inventory assets and vendors, classify data, implement least privilege, backups, update controls, incident-response plans, human approval and tested restoration. Do not authorize autonomous consequential decisions.

Official resources

FTC fraud reporting: ReportFraud; identity theft recovery: IdentityTheft.gov; FBI internet crime: IC3; CISA; NIST AI RMF. For imminent danger use emergency services.

Formulas, assumptions and worked examples — usable without JavaScript
  • Budget: lost revenue = annual revenue × federal share × cut fraction; remaining gap = max(0, loss + additional expense − usable reserves). Sample $100M × 20% × 25% = $5M loss; $2M added cost and $3M available reserves leave $4M.
  • Household: gallons × price increase + food spending × rise fraction. Sample 50 × $1 + $600 × 10% = $110/month or $1,320/year.
  • Lifecycle: initial cost + annual maintenance × years. Sample $5M + $250,000 × 20 = $10M, undiscounted.
  • Incentives: cost ÷ promised jobs; annual local payroll = jobs × local share × annual wage. Sample $5M ÷ 200 = $25,000 per promised job; 200 × 60% × $60,000 = $7.2M payroll, not tax revenue or actual jobs.
  • Cyber: workers × hours × hourly labor cost; add distinct recovery expense. Sample 50 × 24 × $40 = $48,000; add $25,000 for $73,000 total.

All inputs hypothetical. Negative/nonfinite values, invalid percentages or zero jobs are excluded. Do not interpret results as Pueblo forecasts.
