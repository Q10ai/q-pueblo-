Full story and context

The City’s October 20, 2026 release reports 320 units permitted in 2025 and describes Compass Pointe, Riverly and other housing projects at different development stages. These figures do not establish that all the homes are completed, occupied or affordable to every household.

What's being built

In October, the City reported that 320 housing units were permitted in 2025. Its development statement describes Compass Pointe as 192 homes scheduled for 2027 and Riverly as 98 proposed units. Permitting, financing and construction are different steps.

Why people care

Housing is only useful to a household if a completed home is available at an affordable cost and in a location that works for that household. Wages, transportation, utilities and financing can all influence access.

What the evidence establishes

The City described a development pipeline, not a count of occupied or affordable homes. This study does not establish actual rents, completion rates or household eligibility. The City's Housing and Homelessness Guide below provides practical resources.
