How to read these records

Count leader: who or which response has the most votes in the reported geography. This is not a certified winner. An agency's current official, the next officeholder, and the agency's actual policy are three separate questions.

Funding: approval, appropriations, collections, commitments, payments and realized effects are different stages. No forecast is represented as money already spent.

County subtotal warning: Colorado statewide, congressional and regional-district contests shown here may cover far more territory than Pueblo County; the county PDF cannot determine their full outcomes.
