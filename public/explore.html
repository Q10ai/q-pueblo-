Q PUEBLO RESEARCH STANDARD

What every issue record means

Past / present / future

Preserve the original event and source date, establish what is currently documented, then label future choices as scenarios rather than forecasts.

Money is a sequence

Requested, authorized, budgeted, contracted, invoiced, paid, recovered and measured outcomes are separate financial stages.

Connections are not conclusions

Relationships show who had a documented role. They do not, by themselves, establish misconduct, motives, wrongdoing or benefits.

Children, residents and trust

Every packet requires a contextual child/family impact assessment, privacy review, source record and correction mechanism before consequential publication.
