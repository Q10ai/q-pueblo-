Protection and practice

A familiar voice asks for money

Do not rely on the voice or caller ID. Call the person at a number you know; use another trusted contact if needed. If you paid, contact your financial provider promptly. FTC guidance.

A digital service stops working

Use an authorized manual alternative and official contact channels; do not unilaterally restart critical equipment. Maintain accessible offline information.

An automated decision seems wrong

Ask how the decision was made, what correction or appeal is available, and who has human authority. Keep permitted records. Applicable legal rights vary.

An alarming AI-generated claim spreads

Check the date, original source and independent corroboration. Avoid amplifying unverified allegations.

For actual emergencies use official emergency resources; call 911 for immediate danger.

Human and machine time

Software can act before people can deliberate. Before deployment, an accountable operator must define tested, bounded, reversible safety responses. Humans retain authority over consequential decisions, appeals and restart. No self-authorizing agents or live incident dispatch are provided by Q.

Build your own preparedness plan

Choose a risk, an opportunity and an essential service. Identify a responsible person, a safe fallback, a verification method and a review date. Keep your plan privately on paper or your own device. Download the private planning worksheet.
