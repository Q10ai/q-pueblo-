Do the analysis—not just the searching.

Three working, deterministic Q-Pueblo instruments run in your browser. You can assess time and cost, calculate a bounded decision score, or reconcile money stages without paid AI or sending your inputs to Q-Pueblo.

Important: Values are your assumptions. These tools do not fetch official documents, verify civic allegations, predict outcomes, measure AI electricity consumption or issue government decisions. Save the result, then attach sources before treating it as evidence.

EE · Time, labor and operating cost

Compare a manual process with a proposed tool-assisted process. Include human checking time rather than hiding it; energy numbers appear only if you supply Wh/task assumptions.

Choose values, then calculate.

T2Y · Truth-to-Yield decision discipline

Reference normalized scoring from Q10’s controlled EE/T2Y specification. The truth-support and harm gates cannot be offset by high usefulness, speed or enthusiasm. Enter 0–1 estimates, not claims of independent measurement.

Choose bounded assumptions, then evaluate.

Follow the Money · No-double-count stage checker

Enter amounts for one defined program, fund and time period. Proposed, authorized, contracted, paid, recovered and measured benefit represent different stages and must not be summed as if separate taxpayer bills.

A $5 million authorization and a later $3 million payment are not an $8 million authorization.

From planning arithmetic to a real civic report

Choose a source-backed Q-Pueblo report, use its exact fund and period, compare original documents, and propose a correction when the evidence changes. Tools do not replace the source, a competent auditor or lawful public authority.
