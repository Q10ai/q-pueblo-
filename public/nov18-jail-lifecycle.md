# New county jail: capacity, debt and full lifecycle costs

Dated 2026-11-18. Living public research edition; no independent audit or live monitoring. Physical beds are not staffed beds. The new facility’s first-year operations, debt and medical costs must be reconciled with proposed workforce cuts.

## 1. Answer now
County reports the new detention center is operating and first-year performance is being tracked. [D]

## 2. Decision
Determine safely staffed capacity and lifecycle affordability before adding municipal detention demand.

## 3. History
New construction and prior capital obligations contribute to the financial outlook; a project-specific cost bridge is required. [B,L]

## 4. Current position
County reports old detention center mothballing and new facility warranty monitoring. [D]

## 5. Dates
Record opening, warranty deadlines, debt dates and IGA votes separately.

## 6. People
Detainees, employees, families, courts, victims and emergency responders rely on safe operations.

## 7. Children
Review family separation, visits, transport and disruption to children of employees.

## 8. Workers
Track vacancies, training, fatigue and avoidable overtime; do not publish shift vulnerabilities.

## 9. Geography
County owns detention responsibilities; municipal use requires a specific agreement.

## 10. Fairness
Detention care and safety standards apply regardless of charging jurisdiction.

## 11. Authority
Sheriff operates jail; county appropriates; judges make lawful detention and sentencing decisions.

## 12. Decisions
Require authorized staffing and medical leaders to attest capacity by classification and function.

## 13. Rules
Check applicable Colorado jail standards and current inspection findings before drawing compliance conclusions.

## 14. Commitments
Separate construction contracts, financing covenants, warranty obligations and operating contracts.

## 15. Oversight
Review independent inspections, aggregate incidents and corrective actions; do not disclose security-sensitive details.

## 16. Funding
Trace bonds/COPs, pledged revenues and cash-funded construction; do not treat countywide capital totals as jail costs.

## 17. Cost
Build capital, debt interest, food, utilities, medical, staff, maintenance and reserve ledgers by year.

## 18. Approval
An authorized project budget and a signed contract are not the same as final cost.

## 19. Paid
Reconcile payments, change orders, retainage, financing draws and insurance claims to actual records.

## 20. Value
Compare design capacity, safely available capacity, service outcomes and annual lifecycle costs.

## 21. Operations
Bookings require intake, classification, healthcare, transport, supervision and court processing.

## 22. Capacity
Design beds minus blocked/maintenance beds is not enough; staffing and classification limit actual capacity.

## 23. Quality
Measure aggregate safety, medical wait and compliance, including baseline and after changes.

## 24. Reliability
Warranty inspection and preventative maintenance should precede warranty expiry.

## 25. Delays
Delayed hiring, clinical recruitment and repairs may make apparent capacity unavailable.

## 26. Crosswalk
County fiscal recovery and workforce cuts may limit the city’s proposed additional bookings.

## 27. EE
Identify fixed versus variable costs and avoid claiming savings by moving bills between governments.

## 28. Environment
Meter water and energy use against occupancy and weather; verify actual utility charges.

## 29. Stress
Test lower staffing, higher medical demand, delayed debt financing and unusable housing areas.

## 30. T2Y
Plan 1-, 3-, 5- and 10-year renewal costs, warranty exits, staff training and reserve needs.

## 31. Options
Improve utilization without exceeding safety floors; explore eligible diversion and lawful scheduling.

## 32. Tradeoffs
An occupied-bed payment creates county revenue but may not cover incremental medical or staffing expense.

## 33. LL
Pueblo’s existing warranty/old facility tracking is a useful local baseline; extend to audited cost and outcomes. [D]

## 34. RX
Publish cost-per-inmate-day methodology and monthly aggregate staffed capacity; obtain IGA cost allocation.

## 35. Help
Use official sheriff information for custody and services; use emergency authorities for urgent threats. [J]

## 36. Evidence strength
Official dashboard confirms operations; exact current staffing and cost denominators remain absent.

## 37. Contrary evidence
Added bookings may be feasible if spare staffed capacity exists and reimbursement covers net cost; neither is established.

## 38. Unknowns
Current bed utilization, verified design capacity, final project costs, incremental expense and signed IGA.

## 39. Reopen
Updated inspection, occupancy, payroll, financing or agreement; refresh before operational decisions.

## 40. Qified synthesis
The jail is a long-term public asset and operating obligation, not a stand-alone answer to crime or budget pressures.
