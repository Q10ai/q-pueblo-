## The story

Fiber internet is being developed in Pueblo. Intrepid's local project page identifies T-Fiber as an ordering partner and distinguishes planned construction, engineering and permitting, construction, and service availability. Those stages matter: a fiber line in the neighborhood does not mean an individual address can order service.

Construction, right-of-way permits, network ownership, retail service and street restoration involve different responsibilities. The local provider page supplies a construction contact, 1-844-380-8090. Actual address availability, contract terms and restoration acceptance remain unverified in this study.
