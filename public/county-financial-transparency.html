Where are Pueblo County’s 2025 financial records?

The county salary and ACFR archives both end in 2024 on November 18. Missing web publications are documented accessibility gaps, not proof that salary notices were never published or that the state never received an audit. [S1,S2]

Independent educational public-source review, not a certified audit or government finding. Evidence status and proposals are distinguished below; missing information does not prevent publication of available findings.

Findings and processing · Unresolved questions · Original sources · Download structured packets

1. Dated public observation

The financial archive lists 2012–2024 (13 annual reports); salary archive lists 2017–2024 (eight). The salary page retains an introductory notice referring to 2020. Neither archive shows its 2025 annual document. The user supplied six screenshots consistent with these observations. [S1,S2]

2. Public accessibility versus legal compliance

C.R.S. 30-25-111 requires salary information in February for the previous calendar year and August for the previous June. The publication mechanism is normally a legal newspaper. It also covers monthly paid claims and contracts and semiannual treasurer statements. A missing website link does not prove nonpublication in a newspaper. [S3]

3. Audit deadline

For December year-end governments, the audit normally reaches the entity by June 30 and the state by July 31, or a filing-extension request is due by July 31. The state has delinquency and potential tax-withholding processes. Do not infer an extension or enforcement outcome from a website gap. [S4]

4. County's own explanation

The county reports additional accounting work approved September 29, draft statements in the second half of October and audit fieldwork the week of October 26. A dashboard status is not a signed audit opinion or a state filing receipt. [S5]

5. Community signal

A supplied social-media post questioned the missing files and commissioner pay during proposed workforce cuts. Four subsequent images show six distinct visible comments, one partial. Themes include distrust, pay equity, deputy staffing, hiring barriers and retirement eligibility. Nine shares were visible; comments were filtered. This is a qualitative signal, not representative sentiment. No names, profile images or insulting claims are republished.

6. Comparison and alternatives

Douglas County offers year-end 2025 and June 2026 disclosures on its payroll page. Use the comparable disclosure cadence as a practical benchmark, not a rank of all counties. [S6]

7. Audit processing

Inventory each statutory record by entity, fiscal period, due date, channel, original URL, retrieval date and evidence status. Compare archived versions and search state repositories, public newspaper notices and meeting packets before filing requests. Record absent on checked page separately from not published anywhere.

8. Accountability

Commissioners set oversight expectations; finance prepares accounting information; the treasurer maintains fund information; the state auditor oversees filing. The independent CPA evaluates evidence; duties must not be assigned to individuals solely because of their job titles.

9. Impact and child lens

Late public information limits independent review of workforce and service decisions. Housing insecurity, family finances and emergency response effects are possible downstream risks, not outcomes demonstrated by these records.

10. EE / LL / T2Y

Reuse archives and law links across all financial investigations; retain the first recorded absence and later publication dates. Calculate repeated search time and rework instead of assuming savings. Preserve the difference between document date, as-of date and date observed.

11. RX and leap gate

Publish a dated disclosure calendar plus human-readable and CSV/JSON exports. For each item show not due, found, late against normal date, extension verified or not located. Never silently overwrite a past observation. Human review is required before changing a status to violation.

12. Event horizons

Recheck during December 2026 budget deliberations, in February 2027 for 2026 annual payroll, and August 2027 for June payroll. Follow the next annual audit cycle, then compare on-time performance in 2029 and 2031. These are Q review milestones, not county commitments.
