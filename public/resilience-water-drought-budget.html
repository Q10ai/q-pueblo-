Water security through 2031: drought, rights, rates and household budgets

Drought planning means testing reliable supply, legal use, utility costs and household affordability together.

Start with your provider

Pueblo Water and Pueblo West Water are different utilities. Confirm your own bill and service area; do not apply one utility's rates or rules to another. Pueblo West's May 2026 Drought Watch notice and draft efficiency plan are evidence of planning, not a guarantee of current restrictions. Check the Water Department page and any updated notices.

How water rights and supply interact

The Arkansas basin is Colorado Water Division 2. The Southeastern district's basin line diagram identifies major rights and administering districts. A water right, legal use, reservoir storage, physical inflow and reliable delivery under drought are different measures. Obtain actual decrees, enforceable conditions, compact obligations and infrastructure capacities before comparisons. State Water Plan scenarios are planning snapshots, not exact local forecasts.

Household needs and affordability

Pueblo Water lists payment arrangements, CARES and budget billing. Its emergency line is 719-543-2217; customer service 719-584-0250. Ask about a leak-related sewer adjustment and service-line repair eligibility; different parts of the line have different responsibilities. Sewer and stormwater charges are collected by Pueblo Water on behalf of the City, with separate calculations. Verify current fees and eligibility. Pueblo West lists conservation rules and rebate information on its own website.

Financial and supply scenarios

Drought can reduce volumetric revenue while treatment, pumping, repairs and capital needs persist; this is a potential exposure, not an adopted rate change. Use the water balance and household bill tools with your own assumptions. Distinguish annual versus peak demand, water legally available versus physically deliverable, withdrawals versus consumption and return flows. A reserve is a planning constraint, not another use to count twice.

Q Rx and LL

Compare conservation, leak repair, efficient fixtures, appropriate landscaping, lawful reuse and supply acquisition. EPA WaterSense provides product savings estimates; Colorado Water Plan provides regional scenario context. Q recommends stage-gated implementation and measured outcomes, not automatically approving transfers or restrictions.

2027–2031 and child impact

2027 obtain dated firm yield, tariffs, asset condition and sector demand. 2029 test consecutive dry years and rate affordability. 2031 audit service reliability, maintenance and supply commitments. Watch sanitation, schools, heat exposure, housing stability and fire reserves. No deterministic drought prediction, current reservoir level or household eligibility claim.
