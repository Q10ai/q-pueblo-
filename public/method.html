TRUST · METHOD · PRIVACY · OPERATIONS

How Q-Pueblo earns trust.

Q-Pueblo is independent, not a government agency or an accredited official public record. Our public mission is to make civic information easier to understand while preserving truth, provenance, multiple perspectives, human control and the right to correct the record.

Evidence labels

Primary source: ordinance, enacted instrument, agenda, approved statement or official record. Source-attributed: a documented statement by a participant, without independent adjudication. Research scope: title or lead without enough linked evidence to make a claim. Scenario: conditional assumption, never a prediction.

Qification

Every completed Qified packet should identify who, what, when, where, why and how; legal decision authority; funding stages; first-, second- and third-order connections; affirmative and contrary evidence; risks and benefits; child/community impacts; scenarios; EE/T2Y reasoning where supported; recommended next steps; and a dated review ledger.

AI bounds

AI may gather, classify, compare, calculate and draft. It cannot independently govern, spend, contract, adjudicate, approve public claims, or publish unreviewed allegations. Q10 core remains a separately controlled engineering portfolio and is not implied to run on this site.

Counts and freshness

174 issue IDs and 141 source URLs are from an older dated public catalog. 54 source publishers are not a verified government-entity registry. A complete post-November reconciliation remains open. Our present reports are limited to the cited record dates.

Privacy, public input and independence

Static pages perform search and calculations in your browser. When you explicitly email a request, your email provider handles the message. Do not submit private data, minors’ information, passwords or privileged records. Sponsorship and paid research cannot purchase a favorable finding or suppress contrary evidence.

Corrections and review

Send a dated source or challenge to Q10pueblo@gmail.com. A request is not automatic approval. Human reviewers should preserve original versions, record specific changes and provide a supersession record before changing public conclusions.

Ownership, funding and accountability — open disclosures

The public source repository is Q10ai/q-pueblo-. A repository name and an independence statement do not establish legal operator identity, editorial staffing, beneficial ownership, funding sources, conflicts-of-interest declarations or the availability of a dedicated review team. Formal, verifiable disclosures remain to be published. Do not assume an email to the project creates a regulated public-records request or a guaranteed response deadline.

Review and corrections status

The Resident Evidence Desk displays a known source-text inconsistency in the City's mill-levy record. This is an unresolved public research question, not a completed agency correction or an allegation of misconduct. A staffed public submission queue, case receipts, response SLA, published disposition ledger and independent editorial audit are not in operation on this static website.

Current capability register

Working in this static release: historical source/issue browse, 14 source-attributed dated explanatory reports, budget-share and department-comparison calculations from Ordinance 11075, County priority filtering from its August 2026 dashboard, browser research packets, EE/T2Y/lifecycle calculators, local exports, and email drafting. Not operational: live web-search agent, autonomous meeting crawling, server-based contribution database, automatic alerts, resident profiling, live digital twin or self-directed Q10 agents. CI checks are not a production penetration test.

Accessibility and offline pathways

Pages aim for keyboard access, readable typography, responsive layouts and no third-party fonts. Spanish overview is available; full translation and independent accessibility validation remain open. Print reports and request documents through Pueblo public libraries or the relevant official institution when needed.
