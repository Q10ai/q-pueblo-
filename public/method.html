How Q-Pueblo earns trust.

Q-Pueblo is independent, not a government agency or an accredited official public record. Our public mission is to make civic information easier to understand while preserving truth, provenance, multiple perspectives, human control and the right to correct the record.

What a Q-Pueblo investigation should deliver

Start with the useful answer and the story behind it: the facts, dates, people, money, decisions, consequences and choices. Original records, detailed calculations and the research coverage appendix remain available for readers who want to examine them. Material limitations belong in the main story.

How research should be developed

Our research standard is to recover earlier evidence first, find original records and contrary evidence, resolve relevant dates and financial stages, and consider historical, professional, community and non-expert perspectives. Independent verification is required where warranted; AI-assisted perspectives are not testimony from real residents or licensed experts.

Use the same admitted evidence across related issues rather than repeatedly gathering the same records. Recheck when sources or decisions change. Do not publish unsupported findings merely to fill a checklist. If the published library cannot answer a question, say what is missing. The public site searches dated research; it does not execute live research agents or independently certify its findings.

Evidence labels

Primary source: ordinance, enacted instrument, agenda, approved statement or official record. Source-attributed: a documented statement by a participant, without independent adjudication. Research scope: title or lead without enough linked evidence to make a claim. Scenario: conditional assumption, never a prediction.

How Q explains an issue

Start with an answer, then see the evidence, what it means, who is affected and possible solutions. Explore real examples.

AI bounds

AI may gather, classify, compare, calculate and draft. It cannot independently govern, spend, contract, adjudicate, approve public claims, or publish unreviewed allegations. Q10 core remains a separately controlled engineering portfolio and is not implied to run on this site.

Dates and freshness

Each report states when its sources were checked. Agendas, cancellations and emergency notices can change quickly. Rely on the issuing agency for the latest time-sensitive information.

Privacy, public input and independence

Static pages perform search and calculations in your browser. When you explicitly email a request, your email provider handles the message. Do not submit private data, minors’ information, passwords or privileged records. Sponsorship and paid research cannot purchase a favorable finding or suppress contrary evidence.

Corrections and review

Send a dated source or challenge to Q10pueblo@gmail.com. A request is not automatic approval. Human reviewers should preserve original versions, record specific changes and provide a supersession record before changing public conclusions.
