    What a relationship does—and does not—prove

    Q-Pueblo separates an institution’s stated jurisdiction, a recorded payment, a contract, a meeting appearance and an inferred connection. Those are different relationship types. A shared address, campaign support or co-attendance does not establish an improper relationship. Every claim requires a dated provenance record and an opportunity to correct errors.
