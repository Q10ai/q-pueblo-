Recovery gap estimator

Planning estimate: costs minus confirmed nonrepayable assistance and available funds. Pending aid and loans are excluded; this does not estimate eligibility.

Enter your own values and select Calculate.

Project funding-share planner

Federal share applies only to eligible costs; secondary share applies to the remaining eligible costs. Actual signed agreement controls; do not assume approval.

Enter your own values and select Calculate.

Drought supply margin

Use consistent units and time periods, e.g. acre-feet/year. Supply must represent lawful deliverable firm yield. Reserve is additional only if not already excluded.

Enter your own values and select Calculate.

Household bill change scenario

Apply a hypothetical percentage change to your own water and sewer/stormwater amounts. Not an official tariff or forecast; do not assume all charges increase equally.

Water and cost savings

Estimate avoided volumetric charges only, not fixed fees or tier changes. Consult your utility and EPA WaterSense.

Industrial direct water scenario

Annual direct-site consumptive water estimate only if your intensity is truly consumptive liters per kWh of IT electricity. Not a local facility estimate or peak-demand assessment.
