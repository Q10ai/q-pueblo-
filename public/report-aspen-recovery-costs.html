Findings and more detail · Sources

Short answer

Pueblo County's recovery dashboard reports 453 households in temporary lodging, 36 dumpsters and 81 state resource requests. Its September 8 financial detail identifies $321,833.63 in direct County response and recovery costs. These are published snapshots, not a November 14 cumulative total, a current containment update or final cost audit.

The full story

The work after the fire

The County recovery dashboard reports support for 453 households in temporary lodging, 36 dumpsters and 81 state resource requests. These are published counts, not a verified number of households still displaced.

Following the money

September 8 financial detail identifies $321,833.63 of direct County response and recovery costs. A $500,000 grant request was listed as pending MOU and invoicing. State resource costs and County costs are separate; requested grants are not payments received.

Where recovery stands

Recovery depends on safe housing, infrastructure and assistance as well as financial reconciliation. The dashboard is useful for understanding reported actions and locating recovery resources. It does not establish the current hazard, final net cost or current individual eligibility.
