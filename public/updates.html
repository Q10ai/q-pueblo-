What Q does with updates

A learning system, not rumor automation

01

Watch official feeds

Read publisher-provided titles, dates, URLs and updated meeting material. Preserve the source as published.

02

Crosswalk affected issues

Record which institutions, issues and budgets need rechecking. A title match is a lead, not proof of what happened.

03

Release reviewed analysis

Human reviewers examine supporting records and competing explanations before claims or financial updates enter public reports.

No meeting outcome, attendance number or real-time alert is inferred from a feed title. Q-Pueblo currently operates a static data and research layer; the official-feed automation and hosted backend depend on the production deployment and configured credentials.
