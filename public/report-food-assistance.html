Short answer

Pueblo County lists emergency food assistance at no cost for qualifying households. Text FOOD to 1-877-644-3663 to receive an intake survey link. Seniors age 60 or older may also qualify for Everyday Eats; SNAP is a separate application and EBT benefit.

Full story and context

Pueblo County lists emergency food assistance at no cost for qualifying households. Text FOOD to 1-877-644-3663 to receive an intake survey link. Seniors age 60 or older may also qualify for Everyday Eats; SNAP is a separate application and EBT benefit.

If you need food

Pueblo County describes emergency food assistance at no cost to qualifying households. Its published intake is to text FOOD to 1-877-644-3663 for a survey link. Participation in specified assistance programs, including SNAP and Medicaid, or an applicable income route may establish eligibility.

Help beyond one pickup

Everyday Eats offers qualifying people age 60 and older monthly food assistance. SNAP is a separate EBT benefit, available through its own application process. The County lists Colorado PEAK and its department application routes; call 719-583-6160 to request a mailed SNAP application.

What to expect

A published intake route does not guarantee inventory, pickup time or eligibility. Use the official links below to confirm the program that fits your situation. Do not send personal documents to Q.
