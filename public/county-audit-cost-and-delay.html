What audit is Pueblo County paying for — and why is it taking so long?

County reports accounting-close support September 29 and independent fieldwork starting the week of October 26. A separate October 1 financial management engagement has a reported $170,950 ceiling; that is not the independent audit fee. [S1,S2]

Independent educational public-source review, not a certified audit or government finding. Evidence status and proposals are distinguished below; missing information does not prevent publication of available findings.

Findings and processing · Unresolved questions · Original sources · Download structured packets

1. Three distinct consulting scopes

County reports August 18 budget preparation support, September 29 closing/reconciliation and statement compilation support, and October 1 executive/controller support. None is automatically the independent financial statement audit. [S1]

2. What is known about price

Secondary reporting describes 13 weeks at $7,900 for approximately 24 hours/week and 13 weeks at $5,250 for approximately 16 hours/week. Calculated total: $102,700 + $68,250 = $170,950 over 520 approximate hours, or $328.75 per nominal hour. This is a schedule/ceiling, not an invoice or actual cost. [S2,S3]

3. Timeline and bottleneck

The normal audit delivery deadline was June 30; July 31 is the normal state filing or extension-request deadline. A controller joined August 24; closing support was authorized September 29; drafts reached auditors in late October. The county's stated fieldwork start implies preparation was delayed, not that the independent auditor had been working on fieldwork all year. [S1,S4]

4. October governance event

October 2 reporting describes the October 1 CFO demotion, temporary finance supervision and a commissioner saying monthly reports remained unavailable. Attribute this to reporting, not an independent finding of individual culpability. [S2]

5. Audit versus compilation versus forensic review

A compilation helps assemble data; a financial statement audit tests evidence and expresses an opinion; a forensic investigation examines defined allegations or transactions. A grant Single Audit addresses applicable federal awards and compliance. These scopes and fees must stay separate.

6. What public evidence can do now

A desk review can reconstruct authorizations, compare years, recalculate totals, flag late reporting and inspect debt disclosures. It cannot confirm bank balances, completeness of liabilities or authorized signatures without underlying evidence. No CORA request is needed to publish the desk-review findings.

7. Why the system should change

A public-facing status labelled achieved can reflect an intermediate milestone. Track books closed, bank reconciled, schedules compiled, draft delivered, fieldwork started, opinion signed, state accepted and public report published separately. [S1]

8. Continuous assurance pilot

Use export-only financial data, deterministic bank-to-ledger matching and duplicate invoice checks, period-close calendar, exception register and monthly approval. AI can extract or explain, but cannot approve payments or certify financial statements.

9. Audit independence and controls

Separate preparation from independent assurance. Require named accountable owners, auditor independence review, sampling and materiality policy, evidence retention, read-only access and documented exception disposition. Retain a manual fallback.

10. Public cost packet

For each SOW store resolution, vendor, scope, ceiling, amendments, invoices, paid amounts, deliverables, dates, dependencies and acceptance sign-off. No cost summation until overlap and contract identity are reconciled.

11. Workforce and children

Repeated reconciliation consumes staff capacity while budget cuts threaten service continuity. Test whether staff training and process redesign reduce errors without shifting burdens to families or eliminating human review.

12. EE / LL / T2Y

Measure time to close, age of unmatched transactions, corrections per period, nonduplicated consultant costs and staff hours saved after review. Maintain lessons across administrations. Compare the actual monthly close with the target, not the stated intent.

13. RX and leap

Pilot one completed month in parallel using approved local exports. Test totals to source, role segregation, restore/rollback and exception detection. Expand only after finance leaders and independent reviewers accept the results.

14. Event horizons

In December 2026 check completion and actual procurement status. In 2027 track monthly close cadence and the next audit; in 2029 compare recurrence of findings, costs and staff workload. The five-year gate is sustained controls, not merely software purchased.
