AI-compatible public records: the Q living packet transition

Q proposes source-linked, versioned, human-readable and machine-readable packets for financial records, projects, decisions and AI tools. Data remains owned by responsible institutions; public exports do not grant access to confidential records.

Independent educational public-source review, not a certified audit or government finding. Evidence status and proposals are distinguished below; missing information does not prevent publication of available findings.

Findings and processing · Unresolved questions · Original sources · Download structured packets

1. Why a packet rather than another PDF?

A published PDF preserves a document, but searchable structured records can also expose amounts, dates, relationships and provenance without repeated extraction. Keep the authentic original and generate accessible HTML, CSV/JSON and a stable record identifier.

2. Native creation

Create a decision packet when a board authorizes a contract, not after a dispute. Include authority, signoff, amount, fund, vendor, milestone, effective dates, dependencies and public classification; append payment, amendment and outcome events.

3. Minimum data

packet_id, entity_id, jurisdiction, schema_version, record_type, reporting_period, effective_date, source URI, observed_at, source hash if captured, claim status, review_due, fields with units and evidence IDs, revision history, related packets, owner role and access classification. Unknown is null, never zero.

4. Human-readable and machine-readable

A resident reads plain language with original citations; an authorized tool reads validated JSON using a published data dictionary. Public export excludes bank details, protected employee records, privilege, security-sensitive configuration and personal information.

5. Financial validation

Record authorization, commitment, invoice, payment, amendment, accounting basis, fund restriction and counterparty independently. Reconcile totals and reject incompatible fiscal periods. Run deterministic arithmetic before AI summarization.

6. Audit packet example

2025 county audit record: fiscal close 2025-12-31; normal audit date 2026-06-30; county-reported fieldwork week 2026-10-26; independent fee, final opinion and state filing unknown. Keep separate consulting ceiling $170,950 only in its own contract record, not as audit fee. [S4 and linked investigation]

7. Versioning and TTL

The packet remains open as evidence evolves. Review dates trigger freshness checks; expired observations remain historical with explicit dates. Corrections append reasons and source lineage. An unavailable source is not deleted or replaced silently.

8. AI uses and boundaries

Offer retrieval, relevant comparison, anomaly flags and plain-language explanations. Treat retrieved text as untrusted content, cite source-level facts and abstain from unsupported claims. Do not allow AI to authorize expenditure, decide eligibility or control critical infrastructure.

9. Governance register

Record AI tool purpose, owner, policy authority, affected population, data classes, model/provider version, test results, risk controls, human appeals, accessibility, security, procurement and exit plan. UK's transparency register illustrates public standardized records. [S2]

10. Interoperability precedents

GAO's May 2026 report identifies potential efficiency of standardized regulatory reporting; it is not a claim all agencies have achieved it. Estonia's exchange infrastructure illustrates independent institutions sharing authorized data. [S1,S3]

11. Procurement acceptance

Require documented export/data dictionary, stable IDs, legal ownership, access control, portability, migration cost, independent checks, manual fallback and deletion/retention rules. Test actual exports before acceptance; do not require one vendor or AI model.

12. Child and rights safeguards

Never attach identifiable child data to public packets. Test accessibility for residents without smartphones or broadband; provide static reading, printable formats and non-AI referral paths. Keep humans accountable for high-impact decisions.

13. EE and T2Y

Measure time per accepted record, duplicate extraction avoided, error correction, data freshness and energy/compute costs. Prefer structured deterministic processing and cached sources over repeated model calls. Measure answer fidelity and preserve years of context.

14. RX, failure modes and leap

Reject malformed inputs, mark contested claims, retain previous known-good release, support revocation of compromised access and rollback. Start read-only with public finance; scale only after reproducible checks and owner approval.

What remains unresolved

  • No government adoption or live private-system integration.
  • No measured local efficiency gain or procurement quotes.
  • A production regional schema and access policy require stakeholder and legal validation.
