40 research findings

1. Research question

Can the proposed county budget restore recurring balance while preserving essential service capacity? This is a conditional fiscal analysis, not an audit.

2. Status

The Nov. 19 directory had no linked adopted budget. The October proposal is not an enacted appropriation. [S3]

3. Method

Compare stages and fund-level categories; preserve original rounding, separate one-time resources, and distinguish predictions from outcomes.

4. All-funds baseline

2026 budget $255.83M, 2027 initial proposal $217.28M, before targeted General Fund reductions. [S1 p2]

5. Adjusted stage

Subtracting the $9.81M target gives $207.47M, a derived 18.9% decline from the 2026 budget, not an official final all-funds total. [S1 pp2,7]

6. General Fund flows

Reported proposed sources $122.67M and after-target spending $121.40M; county reports $1.26M surplus. The $0.01M displayed arithmetic discrepancy is rounding, not evidence of wrongdoing. [S1 p8]

7. Revenue structure

Property tax $59.23M; sales/use/ownership $37.37M; fees $12.57M; grants $5.88M; other revenue/transfers $7.52M. New debt proceeds zero in proposed 2027. [S1 p4]

8. Revenue risk

Grant decline and absent new debt proceeds explain much of the comparative sources decline; neither is necessarily a recurring tax-base loss. [S1 p4]

9. Capital exposure

Capital outlays across governmental funds increased from $10.9M in 2021 to $118.8M in 2024; compare completed projects and funded obligations before calling planned reductions service cuts. [S2 PDF p2]

10. Marijuana receipts

County reports $14.3M in 2021 and $5.4M in 2025, across restricted and other streams; 2025 unaudited. [S2 PDF p3]

11. Operating cost drivers

Insurance, detention medical and gross health claims rose between 2023 and 2025; test price, utilization, claims and reimbursements separately. [S2 PDF pp4,6]

12. Debt service

Governmental-fund debt service was $9M in 2021 and $19.8M in 2024. The 2027 General Fund starting proposal includes $21.35M debt service. Verify series, due dates, coverage and fund. [S2 PDF p5; S1 p5]

13. One-time financing

The $0.90M projected opening 2027 General Fund balance assumes $7M asset sale and $15M COP financing in 2026. Neither transaction's closing is verified here. [S1 p9]

14. Asset disposition

Request appraisals, sale resolutions, transaction costs, net proceeds, restrictions and collateral terms before assessing value or liquidity.

15. Financing failure sensitivity

If net usable receipts assumed in the opening balance do not arrive, model opening balance deterioration separately from the annual operating result; debt-service consequences must be separately recalculated.

16. Fund restrictions

Do not offset a deficit in one fund using another fund's positive balance without documented legal authority and cash availability.

17. Road and Bridge

October projection: opening -$5.48M, closing -$5.10M. Investigate interfund advances, receivables, cash, legal restrictions and road condition. [S1 p9]

18. Human Services

October projection: closing -$0.50M. County says no targeted personnel cuts in this fund; this does not eliminate the negative projection or establish unchanged access. [S1 p9; S5]

19. E911

Projected ending budgetary balance $4.91M; determine statutory restrictions before any cross-fund analysis. [S1 p9]

20. Reduction method

Targets derive from eligible personnel budgets, not 15% of all spending; independent offices choose how to meet targets. [S2 PDF p11; S5]

21. Sheriff

October reduction target $4.89M, preliminary $46.33M, post-target $41.44M. No direct translation into layoff counts. [S1 p6]

22. Current staffing signal

KRDO Nov. 18 reports a sheriff staff warning about likely layoffs; number and scope had not been decided. Obtain dated final office plan. [S6]

23. Other departments

DA $0.84M; facilities $0.68M; clerk $0.60M; planning $0.49M; IT $0.48M targets. Track workload rather than assuming across-the-board service loss. [S1 p6]

24. Emergency services

Monitor dispatch queue, response-time percentiles, staffing coverage and mutual aid. Any threshold requires legal and operational review.

25. Detention

Track occupancy, mandatory care, vacancies, overtime, staff injury and contract costs; a cut can shift spending instead of reducing it.

26. Courts and prosecution

Track case processing, statutory deadlines, vacancy rates and diversion capacity; separate county, district and municipal authority.

27. Children and families

Track actual food, housing, protection and childcare caseloads and wait times; impacts are hypotheses until service data exist.

28. Nonprofits and CSAC

Verify executed city/county agreements, appropriations, receivables, payments and provider-level continuity before quantifying cost shifts. Related report: report-csac-2027.html.

29. City crossover

City and county budgets are separate. City proposals and county withdrawals may affect shared services; never sum balances or assume a transfer. [S7]

30. Wastewater and utilities

City sewer and regulated electric rates have distinct financing and authority; analyze household burden and emergency demand, not presumed county General Fund liabilities.

31. Reserves

County outlook sets a 10% interim floor and 16.67% stabilization target, distinct from TABOR. Verify the policy denominator and qualified reserves before claiming compliance. [S2 PDF p10]

32. Liquidity

Require a rolling 13-week cash forecast, trustee-payment calendar, aged payables/receivables and interfund balance reconciliation.

33. Stress-test methodology

Annual result in millions = 1.26 − 122.67 × revenue miss fraction − 9.81 × unrealized savings fraction − unexpected cost. Ending balance = 0.90 + annual result − opening financing shortfall. Conditional static model, no probabilities.

34. Sensitivity

Baseline +$1.26M; 1% revenue miss +$0.0333M; 2% miss −$1.1934M; 80% savings delivery −$0.702M; combined 2% miss, 80% savings and $2M cost −$5.1554M. Model does not predict outcomes.

35. Recovery options

Compare procurement, shared services, attrition, preventative maintenance, grant recovery and debt restructuring by net recurring cost and service performance. No savings assumed without evidence.

36. Governance

County reports finance leadership reassignment and planned monthly reporting, financial controls and debt-payment processes. Verify dated outputs and independent review. [S2 PDF pp9,12–14; S5]

37. Public participation

Official calendar lists Dec. 4 final Workday adjustment and Dec. 10 adoption hearing. Recheck notice and location before attending. [S4]

38. Records and audit

Request final budget, amendments, audits, monthly ledgers, debt agreements, appraisals, staffing plans, grant obligations and service data; seek redacted aggregate information.

39. TTL and corrections

Recheck active decisions daily, monthly finance on issuance, structural risks quarterly; stale evidence remains visible with its date. Append corrections with source, reason and reviewer.

40. Conclusion and limits

The proposal offers a narrow conditional surplus, not proof of structural recovery. Full 18.93 MB budget book retrieval failed; no detailed audit, final layoffs, cash validation or financing close verified. Human editorial approval and deployment verification remain required. [S1–S6]
