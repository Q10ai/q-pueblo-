Compare a hypothetical cost

Example only: $1,000 licenses, $200 training, $300 integration, $400 review/support and $100 exit provision equal $2,000 for the same evaluation period. With 100 users, that is $20 per user. This does not include every cost, establish benefits or represent a Pueblo school purchase. Use existing Q cost and resource tools to compare alternatives. Keep all real student and confidential data out of the exercise.
