From one issue to the whole regional picture

  1. Find the issue and its original date, jurisdiction and primary document.
  2. Trace directly named organizations; resolve grouped or uncertain identities.
  3. Look two or three steps outward to find other issues and entities as research leads.
  4. Independently establish any claimed directed link: law, funding, contract, asset, labor, environmental or human effect.
  5. Present alternatives, costs, values, evidence gaps and reversible options without making a governmental decision.
