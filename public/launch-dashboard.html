How QPueblo protects the public record

Source and event dates stay visible. A proposed budget is not a paid invoice; a relationship is not misconduct; a modeled effect is not a measured outcome. Draft packets can grow as evidence arrives without rewriting earlier versions.

Release boundary

October 16, 2026 research register; not live data. Historical snapshots require current-record refresh. Sensitive held assertions, private submissions and autonomous agents are not published.
