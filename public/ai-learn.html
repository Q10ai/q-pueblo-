AI, QIFY & the Future: Learning Center

Learn what AI is, what it can and cannot do, why safety and human agency matter, and how Q10 helps turn public sources into accountable questions, research and options.

What Q-Pueblo offers today: public research records, dated curated reports, source links, browser-local tools and reviewable teaching activities. It does not have a live autonomous city agent network, certified policy decision engine, or validated regional digital twin.

Q10's governed path from question to usable product

  1. Understand the person: their real question, needs, rights, scope and possible consequences.
  2. Find evidence: original records, dates, institutions, contradictions and gaps.
  3. QIFY: crosswalk law, finance, energy, environment, social effects, technology and risks without inventing facts.
  4. Test alternatives: establish baselines, simulations and failure modes, all explicitly labeled.
  5. Deliver useful work: a research report, evidence table, decision aid, contact map, learning guide or Q-RX option—not another unsupported promise.
  6. Human review and exit: people remain in control, can seek corrections and use appropriately portable results.
