Four useful scenarios to investigate

💧Water × Housing × Jobs

What happens to housing and economic-development options if verified water capacity changes?

What evidence is needed?

Water rights, asset capacity, land use, household demand, costs, jobs, restrictions.

A scenario is not an observed result or a forecast guarantee.

🔥Wildfire × Recovery × Health

What recovery priorities would change under different infrastructure, health and household assumptions?

What evidence is needed?

Incident source records, hazards, protection needs, public spending and performance.

A scenario is not an observed result or a forecast guarantee.

🚆Rail × Industry × Public Money

Which costs and dependencies change if a proposed service begins earlier, later or not at all?

What evidence is needed?

Executed rail/host agreements, infrastructure cost, service plans, ridership and money stages.

A scenario is not an observed result or a forecast guarantee.

🤖AI × Energy × Civic Trust

What is the smallest useful AI service with accountable evidence and verifiable safeguards?

What evidence is needed?

Data provenance, security, human review, accessibility, compute/energy and trustworthy baselines.

A scenario is not an observed result or a forecast guarantee.

What makes it a real twin—not a presentation?

  1. Precise geographical, jurisdictional, temporal, system and consent boundaries.
  2. Authorized, versioned, documented source layers for infrastructure, law, events, finance, people and resources.
  3. Calibrated equations, competing models, historical backtesting, uncertainty and residual error.
  4. Privacy, child safety, cybersecurity, energy/attention budgets, maintenance and independent verification.
  5. Governed outputs that distinguish actual observations, estimates, scenarios and unsupported ideas.
