Required nonfinancial review

The Q Effect goes beyond the dollar figure.

People & children

Which neighborhoods, workers, families, schools and future residents receive benefits or carry burdens? Identify absent baseline measurements before asserting an effect.

Authority & rights

Who can authorize, challenge, audit, revoke or amend the action? Which contracts or ordinances control?

Resilience & RX

Under outages, drought, revenue decline or supplier failure, how would service continue and what could be rolled back?

Trust & evidence

Record assumptions, counterexamples and unknowns; a favorable modeled result cannot validate a weak source.
