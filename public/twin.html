Try a hypothetical facility. Use facility-level electrical demand (not just servers), average utilization, electricity price and water consumption per kWh. These assumptions are not measured Pueblo data; the results are illustrative, not a project impact assessment.

Open example values and technical controls

Annual electricity = MW × 8,760 × utilization. Electricity cost excludes demand charges, capital and maintenance. The water result is assumed consumption, not permitted withdrawal, regional availability or measured cooling demand. Real assessment needs utility and site-specific records.

What a full calibrated digital twin requires
  1. Precise geographical, jurisdictional, temporal, system and consent boundaries.
  2. Authorized, versioned, documented source layers for infrastructure, law, events, finance, people and resources.
  3. Calibrated equations, competing models, historical backtesting, uncertainty and residual error.
  4. Privacy, child safety, cybersecurity, energy/attention budgets, maintenance and independent verification.
  5. Governed outputs that distinguish actual observations, estimates, scenarios and unsupported ideas.
