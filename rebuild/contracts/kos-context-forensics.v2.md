# KOS v2 — Deep Context, Educational Story and Forensic Research Contract

Version: QP-KOS-CONTEXT-v2.0
Effective design date: 2026-11-21
Status: clean-rebuild specification, NOT proof of active on-demand source retrieval or generation.
Authority: accountable human editorial and release gate.

## Primary purpose

**Deliver the maximum defensible subject context through an engaging, accurate, educational narrative.**

One subject in → one coherent contextual paper out. A full paper is not a directory, tool menu, glossary dump, template checklist, list of research requests or superficial overview. It must give the supported answer directly to the user in language understandable by a typical tenth-grade student, without losing forensic rigor.

Depth is determined by actual subject complexity and evidence, not fixed section quotas, fabricated content, page-count targets or limited attention to the first matching document.

## Ownership: KOS performs the work

1. **Subject recognition and scope** — resolve the user's exact topic, named institutions and geographic/temporal constraints. Show provenance for identities, do not use display order as identity.
2. **Source discovery and prior-work recovery** — follow connected records, old Q Pueblo research, historical archives, external original sources, registered institutions and user-authorized documents. Old work is a discovery aid; its claims must not be promoted by association.
3. **Evidence forensics** — inspect the original material, record issuer, document version, dates, authority, claim support, contradictions and limitations. Keep fact, allegation, inference, scenario and opinion distinct. Explain what records mean, not just where to find them.
4. **Historical reconstruction (LL)** — develop meaningful origins, decisions, changes, culture, people and consequences across time; keep historical evidence accessible after TTL review.
5. **Entity and system crosswalking** — connect neighborhoods through regional/state/federal organizations, named roles and dated appointments, regulators, workers, funders, contractors, projects, communities and downstream dependencies. Every asserted edge requires claim evidence.
6. **Financial and operational forensics** — distinguish proposed → authorized → awarded → obligated → invoiced → paid → delivered outcome. Reconcile years, denominators, amounts, budgets, public cost, maintenance and lifecycle; do not claim misconduct from an accounting gap.
7. **Consequences and SHOCK** — examine relevant direct, secondary and long-tail effects, including safety, children, livelihoods, environment, civil rights, energy, food, disease, hacking, outages, strikes, funding cuts, drought, fires and shared-failure backups. A planning scenario is never a verified active alert.
8. **Options, accountability and foresight** — compare realistic lawful paths, responsible decision-makers, constraints, costs, side effects, unresolved conflict, RX remedies and LEAP/Twin scenarios. Keep human choice and authority.
9. **Qified narrative production** — compose a continuous story at ~10th-grade readability with explanatory transitions, concise definitions, historical-to-present logic and direct usable conclusions. The main paper must be self-contained.
10. **Derived learning and evidence products** — when substantively supported, create historically sourced timelines, visualizations, institutional charts, financial tables, sources/claims, neighborhood maps, curriculum, archival guides and five-level educational activities. No placeholder product counts or fake completeness.
11. **Integrity and publication lifecycle** — source-to-claim traceability, versioning, corrections, reviewed current facts, privacy/rights protections, human go/no-go publication and rollback. A user-requested provisional on-demand answer is not automatically a published institutional finding.

## Reader experience

A reader opens a subject tile or enters an idea.

- **First screen:** direct answer and an interesting, accurate explanation of why the subject matters in Pueblo.
- **Main paper:** coherent connected prose explaining origins, developments, present situation, institutions and people, numbers and finance, major evidence disputes, impact and feasible futures. No arbitrary number of headings.
- **Relevant evidence in context:** citations near material claims; important original documents, comparable figures, dates and context summarized within the text.
- **Deeper view when useful:** supported source excerpts/locators, claim-by-claim ledger, dated entity network, original records, financial forensic schedules, technical engineering assumptions, alternative calculations, historic versions and audit trails. Complex readers do not need a second inconsistent summary.
- **Practical subject tools:** Find Help, Follow the Money, Check a Claim, Compare Solutions, See Who Decides, Read the Timeline, Explore a Scenario, Learn and Play, or Suggest a Correction — only where an action actually works and fits the subject.

A typical tenth grader can read the main paper; an institutionally experienced reviewer can trace its major claims and calculations to original evidence. These are layers of one knowledge product, not two incompatible research systems.

## Forensic depth for large entities

Scope the investigation to the institution's actual complexity, impact and evidence:
- Identity and predecessor organizations, alias disambiguation and legal/jurisdictional authority.
- Three-level organization: sector → organization → departments, programs, public officers and actual responsible roles.
- Dated governance, voting/appointment/contract authorities, audits, oversight, litigation and regulation.
- Operations, service territory, buildings, infrastructure, technology, staffing, supply chains and failure dependencies.
- Public funds, revenues, contracts, obligations, payment stages, debt, grants and lifecycle costs with financial provenance.
- Effects on workers, residents, families, children, elderly and disabled persons, and environmental/water/energy resources.
- LL historical record, contested sources, measured outcomes, forecast assumptions, SHOCK and independent backups.
- Options and remedies with decision authority, likely tradeoffs, constraints and evaluable outcomes.

This is a **completeness review checklist for internal analysis only**. The public narrative includes what the evidence materially supports, not empty sections for every item.

## Evidence reuse and legacy controls

Prior work is not disposable. Old research may contain rare original references, valid historical findings, useful calculations or neglected context. Recover relevant material and independently examine its underlying sources for the current claim. Every recovered statement receives one of:
- VERIFIED_CURRENT: rechecked for present-tense applicability, dated source support.
- VERIFIED_HISTORICAL: supported for a bounded earlier time.
- CONTESTED: credible sources disagree; explain claims and context.
- CONTEXT_ONLY: investigative lead, not standalone evidence.
- RECHECK_REQUIRED: not safe for current assertion until revisited.
- REJECTED or SUPERSEDED: unsuitable as current evidence; historical lineage can remain.

Do not reuse old numbering, title-based identity merges, fixed 40-section report structures or automatically generated relationship claims. The v2 identity registry and aliases are discovery mappings only until verified.

## Required paper acceptance

- The first paragraphs genuinely answer the user's question.
- The paper provides substantial explanatory **context**, not a superficial summary plus links.
- Historical background is accurate and appropriately sourced; recent facts have actual as-of dates.
- Important assertions, amounts, quotations, authorities and relationships are traceable to supporting source versions.
- Multi-step effects and institutional complexity are covered proportionally, not inflated into unrelated sections.
- No invented scenes, testimony, quotes, amounts, scandals, causes, diagnoses, current emergencies or organizational identities.
- Finance preserves accounting stage and denominators; causal claims require stronger support than correlation.
- Contradictory evidence and important unknowns are explained naturally, not as a large reader homework list.
- Appropriate rights, safety and privacy gates are respected.
- The reader has relevant actions and educational extensions without being diverted into a tools dashboard.
- A human approves any public publication and high-stakes release; new research capabilities must be tested before enabled.

## Implementation boundary

The clean v2 branch contains a new subject identity registry and typed linking contract. This specification defines the KOS production goal, but **the live AI retrieval, independent source-verification service and complete on-demand paper generator have not yet been implemented or deployed in v2**. Do not call this specification an operational engine. Preserve existing public website availability until a tested replacement is approved.
